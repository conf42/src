# Bringing Clarity to gRPC through GoFr : The Role of Observability

A talk at [Conf42 Observability 2025](https://www.conf42.com/obs2025) by [Divya Darshana](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Divya_Darshana) (Open Source Contributor @ GoFr.dev). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Divya_Darshana_clarity_gofr_grpc

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cnx9lixzK2s

## Abstract

Modern application without observability is like driving blindfolded, and gRPC is no different. With our open source Golang Framework, GoFr, we remove that blind spot by embedding distributed tracing, logging, and real-time metrics and default heathchecks, directly into our gRPC server and clients.
