# Platform Engineering for Modern Data Infrastructure

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/platform2025) by [Rahul Joshi](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Rahul_Joshi) (Software Engineer @ Meta). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2025_Rahul_Joshi_modern_data_infrastructure

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-oKAk-3Oufw

## Abstract

Stop wrestling with data platform complexity! Discover proven platform engineering patterns that turn infrastructure headaches into developer self-service wins. Real lessons from years of evolution: avoid vendor traps, scale efficiently, delight users.
