# Vector Embeddings and RAG Demystified

A talk at [Conf42 Machine Learning 2025](https://www.conf42.com/ml2025) by [Veliswa Boya](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Veliswa_Boya) (Senior Developer Advocate @ AWS). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Machine_Learning_2025_Veliswa_Boya_rag_embeddings_vector

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QxwQTa6vWGE

## Abstract

In this talk I demystify the world of vector embeddings and explore their pivotal role in enhancing our interactions with vast data sets, ensuring AI systems can offer personalized, contextually relevant responses.
