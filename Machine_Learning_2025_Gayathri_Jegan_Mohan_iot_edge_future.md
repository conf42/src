# Bringing AI to the Edge: How ML is Powering the Future of IoT

A talk at [Conf42 Machine Learning 2025](https://www.conf42.com/ml2025) by [Gayathri Jegan Mohan](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Gayathri_Jegan_Mohan) (Software Engineer @ Microsoft). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Machine_Learning_2025_Gayathri_Jegan_Mohan_iot_edge_future

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NMTr3WnmXkg

## Abstract

By 2025, 75% of enterprise data will be processed at the edge, revolutionizing AI-powered IoT. Discover how ML deployment is transforming smart cities, healthcare, and manufacturing, enabling real-time intelligence and thereby powering the future of IoT.
