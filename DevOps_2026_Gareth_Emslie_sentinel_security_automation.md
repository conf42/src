# From Zero to a DevSecOps Hero: Extending Microsoft Sentinel

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Gareth Emslie](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Gareth_Emslie) (Senior Product Manager & Tech Evangelist @ Dynatrace). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Gareth_Emslie_sentinel_security_automation

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pOvWKjPAxHA

## Abstract

Become a DevSecOps hero with Microsoft Sentinel. Learn how to impress your SecOps colleagues by building custom connectors, analytics rules, and playbooks; enhancing detection and remediation capabilities, and shortening the time required to detect and mitigate attacks.
