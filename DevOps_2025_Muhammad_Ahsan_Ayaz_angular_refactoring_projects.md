# From Patchwork to Picasso: Refactoring 80+ Angular projects and what went wrong

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/devops2025) by [Muhammad Ahsan Ayaz](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Muhammad_Ahsan_Ayaz) (Software Architect @ Scania Group). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2025_Muhammad_Ahsan_Ayaz_angular_refactoring_projects

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iPaNi3sqFoQ

## Abstract

Ever feel like your codebase is more Frankenstein's Monster than a masterpiece? Juggling 180+ Angular projects can lead to inconsistencies, inefficiencies, and pure chaos. But what if there was a way to transform that patchwork into a beautiful, unified solution?
Find out how in the talk.
