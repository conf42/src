# Modern ERP as the Platform Backbone for Scalable, AI-Driven Supply Chains

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/platform2025) by [Vinod Nagarahalli](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Vinod_Nagarahalli) (Director of IT @ Illumina). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2025_Vinod_Nagarahalli_chains_backbone_supply

## Abstract

Legacy ERP is killing your AI ambitions. Learn how platform engineering can modernize ERP into a unified, composable backbone unlocking AI-driven forecasting, predictive maintenance, and autonomous planning. If you're building intelligent supply chains, this is your blueprint.
