# Cloud-Native Enterprise Architecture for Financial Risk Governance: Integrating Data, Compliance, and AI-Powered Fraud Detection

A talk at [Conf42 Kube Native 2025](https://www.conf42.com/kubenative2025) by [Sreenivasulu Gajula](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sreenivasulu_Gajula) (Principal Full Stack Engineer @ Osmania University). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Kube_Native_2025_Sreenivasulu_Gajula_fraud_governance_finance

## Abstract

Discover how cloud-native enterprise architecture can revolutionize financial risk governance—unifying data, automating compliance, and unleashing AI to combat fraud. Learn how to build secure, scalable, real-time platforms that thrive under pressure.
