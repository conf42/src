A talk at [Conf42 Prompt Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/prompt2024) by [Sabarinath Viji Selvam](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sabarinath_Viji_Selvam) (Product Management @ daily.dev). ## Abstract

AI is turning product management upside down. Imagine using AI to draft PRDs, create mockups, and run surveys in a fraction of the time, while machine learning predicts user behavior. I’ll show you how AI helps product teams move faster, launch smarter, and create personalized user experiences.
