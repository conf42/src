# Keeping High-Traffic Systems Running When Things Go Wrong

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Justice Eziefule](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Justice_Eziefule) (Co-Founder @ Metastable Labs). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Justice_Eziefule_availability_resilience_operations

## Abstract

Outages don’t start with chaos. They start quietly. I’ll share hard-earned lessons from running high-traffic systems, what actually breaks in production, and how teams keep services reliable under real-world pressure.
