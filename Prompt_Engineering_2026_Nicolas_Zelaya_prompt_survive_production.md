# Your Agent Passed the Prompt Test. Will It Survive Production?

A talk at [Conf42 Prompt Engineering 2026](https://www.conf42.com/prompt2026) by [Nicolas Zelaya](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Nicolas_Zelaya) (Director, Product Management @ Harness). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2026_Nicolas_Zelaya_prompt_survive_production

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HDsv3o9Pk54

## Abstract

A great prompt doesn't guarantee a reliable AI agent. What happens when your agent encounters unexpected inputs, real users, changing environments, or failures in production?

In this session, we'll explore why prompt testing alone isn't enough and how developers can test, monitor, and improve AI agents for real-world conditions. Learn how to move beyond getting the right response to ensuring your agents behave reliably, safely, and predictably in production.
