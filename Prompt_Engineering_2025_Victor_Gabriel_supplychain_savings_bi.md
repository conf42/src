# Power BI & Prompt Engineering: Driving $30M in Supply Chain Savings at Kellogg’s

A talk at [Conf42 Prompt Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/prompt2025) by [Victor Gabriel](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Victor_Gabriel) (GEP Worldwide @ GEP Worldwide). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Victor_Gabriel_supplychain_savings_bi

## Abstract

Discover how Kellogg’s unlocked $30M in supply chain savings by combining Power BI analytics with prompt engineering. Learn how AI-driven insights transform data into action, delivering efficiency, agility, and massive ROI in global manufacturing.
