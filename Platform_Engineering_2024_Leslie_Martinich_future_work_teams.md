# The Future of Work: Engaged Leaders and Empowered Teams

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/platform2024) by [Leslie Martinich](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Leslie_Martinich) (Founder & President @ Competitive Focus). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2024_Leslie_Martinich_future_work_teams

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aLo-5yB0uWs

## Abstract

Discover how to become an engaged leader and create a trusted workplace. Using real stories from the software industry, this talk equips professionals with practical skills to foster trust, enhance communication, and empower teams, driving retention and productivity in the virtual environment.
