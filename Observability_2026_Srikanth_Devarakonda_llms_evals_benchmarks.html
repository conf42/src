From Benchmarks to Business Impact: Evaluating Generative AI in Production

Video size:

Abstract

As generative AI systems transition from experimental pilots to production environments, traditional evaluation methods often limited to static benchmarks fail to reflect real-world performance. This session introduces a practical, data-driven evaluation framework that connects model behavior to observable system performance and business outcomes. We examine key evaluation dimensions such as factual accuracy, grounding, safety, and task completion, using measurable signals like hallucination rates, response relevance, user feedback, and task success metrics. Attendees will learn how combining automated evaluation pipelines with human-in-the-loop validation improves coverage while capturing nuanced insights for complex and open-ended outputs. The session also explores domain-specific evaluation approaches across use cases like customer support, content generation, and decision support systems. We highlight techniques to detect and mitigate hallucinations, bias, and robustness issues under varying inputs, improving consistency and reliability. Finally, we present a continuous evaluation pipeline integrated into the AI lifecycle, emphasizing monitoring, traceability, and governance. By incorporating feedback loops and observable signals, organizations can ensure auditability and responsible AI adoption. By aligning evaluation strategies with real user interactions and business KPIs, this session equips practitioners to move beyond benchmarks toward building trustworthy, scalable, and production-ready generative AI systems.
