# Transforming Borrower Engagement with a Mobile-First Loan Application Platform

A talk at [Conf42 Cloud Native 2025](https://www.conf42.com/cloud2025) by [Ravi Chandra Chinta](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Ravi_Chandra_Chinta) (Principal Salesforce Developer & Architect @ Temple View Capital). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Cloud_Native_2025_Ravi_Chandra_Chinta_loans_engagement_mobilefirst

## Abstract

Discover how we transformed lending with a mobile-first borrower portal that slashed loan processing times by 30%, boosted customer satisfaction by 35%, and cut support calls by 40%. Learn actionable strategies to modernize platforms, optimize UX, and deliver superior digital experiences.
