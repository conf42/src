# How to Monitor your Monitoring

A talk at [Conf42 Incident Management 2024](https://www.conf42.com/im2024) by [Mathias Palmersheim](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Mathias_Palmersheim) (Solution Engineer @ VictoriaMetrics). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Incident_Management_2024_Mathias_Palmersheim_27_monitoring_monitoring_how

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gewW_PZ-_Ug

## Abstract

If your monitoring system falls over in the middle of the night does your team get paged? I hope the answer is yes, but if it isn't this talk will provide simple cost effective solutions to get started, and even if your monitoring is monitored I can provide tips to help improve your existing setup.
