# Dragon Copilot: AI-Powered Clinical Assistant Transforming Healthcare Workflows and Patient Care

A talk at [Conf42 Kube Native 2025](https://www.conf42.com/kubenative2025) by [Tharun Nallamothu](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Tharun_Nallamothu) (Senior Software Engineer @ Microsoft). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Kube_Native_2025_Tharun_Nallamothu_copilot_clinical_workflows

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DRVcilPzgzQ

## Abstract

Dragon Copilot is an artificial intelligence assistant that streamlines clinical documentation processes, enhancing patient outcomes and productivity. It leverages technologies such as ambient recording, natural language comprehension, and voice dictation to solve problems.
