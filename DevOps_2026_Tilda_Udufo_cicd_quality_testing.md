# Green Checkmarks, Red Flags: What CI/CD Can't Catch

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Tilda Udufo](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Tilda_Udufo) (Organizer @ Outreachy). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Tilda_Udufo_cicd_quality_testing

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1-vUdEJpvvU

## Abstract

You rely on CI/CD, but passing tests don't guarantee quality. Drawing from open source at scale and incidents like XZ Utils, learn what automation misses and how to design review practices and cultural safeguards that work alongside your pipelines.
