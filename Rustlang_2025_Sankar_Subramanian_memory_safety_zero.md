# AI-Powered Machine Vision in Rust: Transforming EV Manufacturing Quality & Automation

A talk at [Conf42 Rustlang 2025](https://www.conf42.com/rustlang2025) by [Sankar Subramanian](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sankar_Subramanian) (Staff Engineer @ Lucid Motors). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Rustlang_2025_Sankar_Subramanian_memory_safety_zero

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47iC6_1xywU

## Abstract

Watch Rust detect microscopic battery defects at 1000 FPS, prevent million-dollar recalls with zero-copy vision pipelines, and power AI that never crashes on the factory floor. Real systems, real impact—see how memory safety saves Tesla-scale production lines!
