# How to Empower Developers to Troubleshoot K8s Independently

A talk at [Conf42 Site Reliability Engineering 2021](https://www.conf42.com/sre2021) by [Itiel Shwartz](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Itiel_Shwartz) (CTO @ Komodor). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_2021_Itiel_Shwartz_troubleshoot_K8s_independently

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yuoTwL117aY

## Abstract

In the “good old days”, Ops/IT teams were responsible for handling issues when applications crash. In the world of microservices, however, developers are required to take on a bigger role in identifying and fixing issues in production, sometimes without having proper tools, privileges or even training.
So how can we empower developers to troubleshoot efficiently and independently?

Join us as Itiel Shwartz, co-founding CTO at Komodor, discusses:

* How to turn developers into k8s troubleshooting experts
* What culture and mindset changes are needed to succeed
* The link between chainge management and troubleshooting
