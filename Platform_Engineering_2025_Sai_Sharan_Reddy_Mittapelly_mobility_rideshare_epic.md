A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/platform2025) by [Sai Sharan Reddy Mittapelly](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sai_Sharan_Reddy_Mittapelly) (Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University). ## Abstract

Building fault-tolerant platforms handling 6.5M daily patient records? Learn how we engineered Epic-rideshare integration with <3s latency, 99.97% uptime, and HIPAA compliance. Real microservices war stories from healthcare's most critical systems!
