# Demystifying Compliance-by-Design: Architecting Regulatory-Resilient Robotics and Financial Systems

A talk at [Conf42 Robotics 2025](https://www.conf42.com/robotics2025) by [Sneha Nallapu](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sneha_Nallapu) (Senior Software Engineer @ J.B. Hunt Transport Services, Inc.). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Robotics_2025_Sneha_Nallapu_compliance_design_finance

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zJ216IULjYs

## Abstract

Robotics in finance is under mounting regulatory pressure. Learn how compliance-by-design powered by audit trails, bi-temporal data, and adaptive workflows transforms compliance from a burden into a structural advantage for resilient automation.
