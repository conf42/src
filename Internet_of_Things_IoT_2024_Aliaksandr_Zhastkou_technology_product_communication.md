# New technologies in product communications

A talk at [Conf42 Internet of Things (IoT) 2024](https://www.conf42.com/iot2024) by [Aliaksandr Zhastkou](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Aliaksandr_Zhastkou) (Product Owner @ Yandex). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Internet_of_Things_IoT_2024_Aliaksandr_Zhastkou_technology_product_communication

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U9tA1muZI0A

## Abstract

New technologies are revolutionizing product communications, from AI chatbots to IoT devices. These innovations enable real-time, personalized interactions, automate tasks, and provide deeper insights, enhancing customer engagement and loyalty.
