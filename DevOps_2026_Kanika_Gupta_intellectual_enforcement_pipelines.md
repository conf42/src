# ML-Driven IP Detection at Scale: CI/CD Meets Cloud-Native Enforcement

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Kanika Gupta](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Kanika_Gupta) (Software Development Engineer @ Amazon). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Kanika_Gupta_intellectual_enforcement_pipelines

## Abstract

Building production-grade IP enforcement systems at scale requires more than ML—it demands battle-tested DevOps. Learn event-driven architectures, observable pipelines, and cloud-native practices that catch violations in real-time while keeping your team sane.
