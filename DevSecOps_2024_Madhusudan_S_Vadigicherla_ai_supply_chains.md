A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2024](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2024) by [Madhusudan S Vadigicherla](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Madhusudan_S_Vadigicherla) (Director, Supply Chain Systems @ Integra Lifesciences). ## Abstract

Discover how AI and DevSecOps are revolutionizing supply chains to tackle disruptions head-on! Learn how combining predictive analytics, automation, and secure development practices can boost resilience, slash response times, and optimize resources. Get ready to future-proof your supply chain!
