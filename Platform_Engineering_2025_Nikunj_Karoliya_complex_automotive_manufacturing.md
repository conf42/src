# Platform Engineering Principles in Complex Systems: Lessons from Automotive Manufacturing Transformation

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/platform2025) by [Nikunj Karoliya](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Nikunj_Karoliya) (Lead engineer @ Pebble Mobility). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2025_Nikunj_Karoliya_complex_automotive_manufacturing

## Abstract

Discover how automotive giants apply platform engineering principles to manage complexity at massive scale. Learn proven strategies for component reuse, interface design, and automation that you can apply to your software platforms today
