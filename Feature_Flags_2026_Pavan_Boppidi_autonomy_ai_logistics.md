# Feature-Flagged Autonomy: Confidence-Gated Human-AI Logistics Systems

A talk at [Conf42 Feature Flags 2026](https://www.conf42.com/featureflags2026) by [Pavan Boppidi](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Pavan_Boppidi) (STAFF SOFTWARE ENGINEER @ WALMART). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Feature_Flags_2026_Pavan_Boppidi_autonomy_ai_logistics

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h6ugmcYyivY

## Abstract

As organizations adopt agentic AI, a key challenge is introducing autonomous decision-making safely while maintaining governance, compliance, and human oversight. This session presents a cloud-native framework for autonomous logistics that combines confidence-gated Human-in-the-Loop collaboration with feature-flag-driven operational control, enabling teams to deploy autonomous capabilities incrementally rather than through high-risk, all-or-nothing transitions.  The architecture integrates serverless infrastructure, edge computing for safety-critical decisions, multi-agent reinforcement learning, and a governance-first control layer. At its core is a dynamic decision-routing mechanism that assigns authority between AI agents and human operators based on real-time confidence evaluation instead of static rules. Feature flags are used to progressively enable, test, monitor, and roll back autonomous behaviors across operational workflows, providing a practical mechanism for reducing deployment risk while maintaining operational continuity.  The session explores architectural decisions, implementation patterns, and governance controls that support safe rollout of agentic systems in production environments. It addresses two common enterprise challenges: unreliable autonomous decisions in unfamiliar conditions and the emergence of ungoverned "shadow AI" systems operating outside established compliance frameworks. The framework incorporates policy enforcement, auditability, controlled system interaction, and decision monitoring directly into the operational workflow.  Attendees will learn how confidence-based decision routing and feature-flag strategies can work together to govern autonomous systems, improve operational resilience, and maintain trust in human-AI collaboration. The talk also provides a practical roadmap for organizations seeking to evolve from fragmented digital operations to governed autonomous systems while balancing innovation, compliance, and business risk.  Key takeaways include practical approaches for progressive rollout of AI capabilities, governance patterns for agentic systems, and lessons for managing autonomy in complex, real-world operational environments.
