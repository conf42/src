# AI-Driven Risk-Aware Decision-Making for Scalable Reliability Systems

A talk at [Conf42 Remix 2026](https://www.conf42.com/remix2026) by [Oreoluwa Omoike](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Oreoluwa_Omoike) (Site Reliability Engineer @ JPMorgan Chase & CO). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Remix_2026_Oreoluwa_Omoike_driven_risk_aware

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4BELqysbNsA

## Abstract

Resilience in Site Reliability Engineering encompasses more than just failure detection; it also includes the ability to make informed choices amidst uncertainty. This talk delves into how AI-driven, risk-aware decision-making extends automation, optimizes risk management, and guarantees the construction of scalable, high-reliability systems.
