# How to kickstart as iOS developer

A talk at [Conf42 Mobile 2022](https://www.conf42.com/mobile2022) by [Ivan Besarab](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Ivan_Besarab) (iOS Team Lead @ Innit.inc). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Mobile_2022_Ivan_Besarab_iOS_background_modes_timeframes_availability

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sgrN905N0tA

## Abstract

In the talk, Ivan will address the following:
- How to kickstart as an iOS dev today?
- What are the best goals for beginners? 
- What is the difference between private schools and public courses?
- Is it worth it to be an iOS dev?

## Key takeaways

- Ivan: I'm going to tell you how to kickstart your IELTS career. He says there are three steps: education, GitHub and first job. Ivan: Record your interview to get back to any question at any time. It's important to have a good supervisor.
