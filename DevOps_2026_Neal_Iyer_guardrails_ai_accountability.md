# Guardrails First: Designing Safe, Scalable, and Accountable AI Agents for Security Operations

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Neal Iyer](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Neal_Iyer) (Director of Product Management @ Splunk - a Cisco company). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Neal_Iyer_guardrails_ai_accountability

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89NFqpH865M

## Abstract

Discover how to build trustworthy AI agents for your SOC with a “Guardrails First” approach—boosting triage speed and accuracy without sacrificing safety, compliance, or human control.
