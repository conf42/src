# Managing Vendor Incidents

A talk at [Conf42 Incident Management 2024](https://www.conf42.com/im2024) by [Mandi Walls](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Mandi_Walls) (DevOps Advocate @ PagerDuty). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Incident_Management_2024_Mandi_Walls_25_vendor_incidents_management

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P8dsVCjJ7MU

## Abstract

Your service is unavailable but it's not your fault! Your vendor is having an incident that is impacting your products. That's important information, but your customers don't really care. What can your team do before and during vendor incidents to better manage customer expectations?
