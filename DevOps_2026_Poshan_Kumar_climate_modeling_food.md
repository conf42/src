# Generative AI Workflows for Climate Risk Modeling in Food Systems

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Poshan Kumar](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Poshan_Kumar) (Managing Consultant - SAP Transformations and Operations @ Senior Lead at Kyndryl). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Poshan_Kumar_climate_modeling_food

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wIp392eKg7s

## Abstract

Generative AI can now simulate climate risks, recommend local adaptations, and produce clear briefs for decision-makers. Learn how to integrate these capabilities into scalable DevOps workflows that strengthen climate resilience across food systems.
