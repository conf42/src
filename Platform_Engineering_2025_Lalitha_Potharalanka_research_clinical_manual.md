A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/platform2025) by [Lalitha Potharalanka](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Lalitha_Potharalanka) (Senior Appian Developer @ FDA). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2025_Lalitha_Potharalanka_research_clinical_manual

## Abstract

We turned clinical trial chaos into a low-code platform that saves lives faster. Massive paperwork reduction, rapid ROI, perfect compliance—in the most regulated industry on earth. Learn how we built platforms that doctors love and regulators approve. Real stories, real impact.
