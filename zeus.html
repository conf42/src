ADP, Amazon, AuthZed, AWS, Cloudthrill, Denk Solutions, Dwelly, DXC Technology, Expedia Group, General Reinsurance, Harness, Meta, Microsoft, OGD ICT-diensten, Olo din, Perelyn, Revolut, Snowflake, Social Discovery Group, Solea AI, Tehpotok, Walmart

Keynote

Shipping with Context: Knowledge Graphs as the Backbone of AI-First Software Delivery

AI is changing how software gets written, but writing code faster is only part of the challenge. The harder question is whether AI can understand what that code means inside the context of an organization: the services it touches, the risks it introduces, the teams responsible for it, the policies it must follow, and the production systems it may impact. In this talk, we’ll explore why...
