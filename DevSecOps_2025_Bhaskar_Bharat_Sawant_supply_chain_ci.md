# Supply Chain Defense by Default — SBOMs, SLSA, and Provenance in CI/CD

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2025) by [Bhaskar Bharat Sawant](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Bhaskar_Bharat_Sawant) (Lead Engineer @ Cornerstone Building Brands). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2025_Bhaskar_Bharat_Sawant_supply_chain_ci

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=28OftT0yHhg

## Abstract

Lock down your software supply chain without slowing delivery. Automate SBOMs, enforce provenance and attestations, adopt hermetic builds, and verify artifacts at deploy—so every release is traceable, trusted, and compliant by default.
