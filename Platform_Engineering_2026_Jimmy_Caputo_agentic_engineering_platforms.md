# Agentic Engineering Platforms

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2026](https://www.conf42.com/platform2026) by [Jimmy Caputo](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Jimmy_Caputo) (Product Builder and Leader @ Harness). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2026_Jimmy_Caputo_agentic_engineering_platforms

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wmfh0QdAvyM

## Abstract

What does a platform need when AI agents start writing code, running tests, and shipping changes? This talk looks at how engineering platforms can give agents the context, tools, and guardrails they need to work effectively. Jimmy will discuss how developer workflows, observability, and platform design can help teams adopt agents while keeping engineers in control.
