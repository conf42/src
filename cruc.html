Conf42 Golang 2021 is a free online conference for engineers and practitioners. All talks premiere on YouTube on the event day and stay available on demand, so you can watch them at your own pace. Here is who is presenting and what the talks are about.

Companies that presented:

Aiven.io, Apple, AWS, DEVotion, Edgeless Systems, Envato, EXOP, Facebook, Google, Hazelcast, Hover Developer Services, IBM, InfraCloud Technologies, Kudelski Security, last9.io, Les-Tilleuls.coop, Magic, Mattermost, MTS Cloud, PSV, Restorepoint, Stone Creek Coaching, Walmart, Weave, Wix
