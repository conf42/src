A talk at [Conf42 Prompt Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/prompt2024) by [Kirill Mozheykin](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Kirill_Mozheykin) (Private Broker/Ex-CPO @ Kalinka Group). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2024_Kirill_Mozheykin_metasearch_realestate_property

## Abstract

Discover how a meta-search platform, enhanced with machine learning, consolidates real estate listings from multiple sources into a single, accurate feed. It eliminates duplicate entries, personalizes searches, and provides full market visibility for consumers and businesses alike.
