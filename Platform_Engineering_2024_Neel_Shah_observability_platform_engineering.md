# Importance of Observability in Platform Engineering

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/platform2024) by [Neel Shah](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Neel_Shah) (Developer Advocate @ Middleware). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2024_Neel_Shah_observability_platform_engineering

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x3NJBn-W0To

## Abstract

By correlating metrics, logs, and traces, platform teams can optimize resource allocation, accelerate troubleshooting, enhance developer experience, improve reliability, and make data-driven decisions for platform evolution.
