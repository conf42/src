# Resilience Engineering for Secure and Fault-Tolerant Enterprise Systems

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2025) by [Raghavendra Reddy Kapu](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Raghavendra_Reddy_Kapu) (Quality Performance Engineer @ Akshaya Inc). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2025_Raghavendra_Reddy_Kapu_resilient_system_secure

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sG_nE1W5nA0

## Abstract

Learn how to build enterprise systems that stay secure, reliable, and fast no matter what breaks. Discover proven fault-tolerance patterns, chaos engineering tactics, and real-world DevSecOps strategies to prevent downtime, stop cascading failures, and keep your systems resilient at scale.
