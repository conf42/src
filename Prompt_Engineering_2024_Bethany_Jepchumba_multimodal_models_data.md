# Inside Multimodal Models

A talk at [Conf42 Prompt Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/prompt2024) by [Bethany Jepchumba](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Bethany_Jepchumba) (Cloud Advocate @ Microsoft). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2024_Bethany_Jepchumba_multimodal_models_data

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QQRhclG5M10

## Abstract

In this session, we will dive into the intricacies of multimodal AI, exploring its mechanisms and the necessity for such advanced systems in today's digital landscape. We'll discuss how these models process and analyze diverse data streams to create a cohesive understanding of user inputs.
