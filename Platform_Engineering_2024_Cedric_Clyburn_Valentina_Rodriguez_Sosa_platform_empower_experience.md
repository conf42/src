# Breaking Down the Principles of Platform Engineering: How to Empower Developer Experience

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/platform2024) by [Cedric Clyburn](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Cedric_Clyburn) (Senior Developer Advocate @ Red Hat) and [Valentina Rodriguez Sosa](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Valentina_Rodriguez_Sosa) (Principal Technical Marketing Manager @ Red Hat). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2024_Cedric_Clyburn_Valentina_Rodriguez_Sosa_platform_empower_experience

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LCSlQ9Qs8gU

## Abstract

Building an app? It sounds simple, right—just open my IDE and start coding. But what about the container runtime, registry, YAML for deploying to Kubernetes, CI/CD, and more? Platform engineering is how we enable organizations to manage this complexity, abstract it, and let developers continue to code.
