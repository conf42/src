# Principles of Team Topologies Applied to Platform Engineering

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/platform2024) by [Cansu Kavili Ornek](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Cansu_Kavili_Ornek) (Associate Principal Specialist Solution Architect @ Red Hat) and [Val Yonchev](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Val_Yonchev) (Head of Customer Success @ Team Topologies). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2024_Cansu_Kavili_Ornek_Val_Yonchev_topologies_principles_engineering

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g1QRPtTDgDQ

## Abstract

Building platforms for automotive embedded systems may feel different from those for high-frequency trading algorithms or highly secure government services. However, certain patterns and principles consistently distinguish those who will excel from those who will be mediocre at best.
