# Operationalizing AI in Finance: MLOps for Scalable BI and Real-Time Analytics

A talk at [Conf42 MLOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/mlops2025) by [Mallikarjun Ramasani](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Mallikarjun_Ramasani) (Software Engineer @ Barclays). Page: https://www.conf42.com/MLOps_2025_Mallikarjun_Ramasani_operationalizing_ai_finance

## Abstract

Deploying ML into production is hard—especially in finance. Learn how to scale AI-powered BI systems with real-world MLOps practices, from data pipelines to monitoring, model drift, and governance.
