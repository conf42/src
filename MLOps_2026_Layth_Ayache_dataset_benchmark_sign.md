# No Dataset, No Benchmark: MLOps for Lebanese Sign Language

A talk at [Conf42 MLOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/mlops2026) by [Layth Ayache](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Layth_Ayache) (Senior AI Systems & Web Engineer | Technical Lead @ Achour Holding). Page: https://www.conf42.com/MLOps_2026_Layth_Ayache_dataset_benchmark_sign

## Abstract

There was no Lebanese Sign Language dataset to download, so we built one: 40,000+ samples collected with Deaf community members, volunteers and NGOs. This talk is about the unglamorous MLOps underneath: rejecting bad landmark extractions before they reach training, models that are allowed to say "I don't know", and reading a 98% internal result without fooling yourself.
