# Things Fall Apart: Navigating Managed Databases for Over a Decade as a Non-DBA

A talk at [Conf42 Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) 2025](https://www.conf42.com/sre2025) by [Renato Losio](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Renato_Losio) (Principal Cloud Architect @ Funambol). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2025_Renato_Losio_things_fall_apart

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FYaK3g5uj8g

## Abstract

Learn to navigate database benchmarks wisely! From crashing managed instances to skyrocketing storage costs, I'll share hard-earned lessons from a decade of managing production databases on the cloud without DBA expertise.
