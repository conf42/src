# Platform Engineering and Digital Transformation: Bridging the Gap

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/platform2025) by [Manik Kashikar](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Manik_Kashikar) (Principal Consultant @ Thoughtworks) and [Prachita Kashikar](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Prachita_Kashikar) (Advocate for Digital Transformation). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2025_Manik_Kashikar_Prachita_Kashikar_gap_bridging_digital

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QiqItGyy_iw

## Abstract

Digital transformation demands robust, scalable, and flexible platforms that can adapt to ever-changing business needs. This presentation will demystify platform engineering by highlighting its pivotal role in driving digital transformation initiatives.
