# Privacy-Aware AI: Advancing Predictive Analytics in Financial Services

A talk at [Conf42 Machine Learning 2025](https://www.conf42.com/ml2025) by [Raghavender Puchhakayala](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Raghavender_Puchhakayala) (Senior Member IEEE @ JPMorgan Chase). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Machine_Learning_2025_Raghavender_Puchhakayala_analytics_predictive_networks

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BiB8Yk4zjW4

## Abstract

Financial services are being redefined by Privacy-Aware AI—unlocking powerful predictive insights while protecting data and meeting strict compliance. With tools like federated learning and differential privacy, we’re not just innovating—we’re building trustworthy, responsible AI for the future of finance.
