A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2024](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2024) by [Clifton Reddy](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Clifton_Reddy) (Web Consultant - Manager @ American National). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2024_Clifton_Reddy_green_code_sustainable

## Abstract

Join me for an electrifying exploration of how sustainable software practices can revolutionize DevOps! In this engaging session, we’ll delve into strategies aimed at enhancing code efficiency, minimizing resource consumption, and aligning your organization with vital corporate social responsibility goals.
