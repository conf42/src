# Building Event-Driven AI Agents for Proactive SAP Incident Management

A talk at [Conf42 AI Agents 2026](https://www.conf42.com/agents2026) by [Pon Murugesh Devendren](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Pon_Murugesh_Devendren) (Manager @ Deloitte). Page: https://www.conf42.com/AI_Agents_2026_Pon_Murugesh_Devendren_sap_incidents_events

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bpD3D8ZEqUU

## Abstract

Modern SAP environments continuously generate operational events ranging from integration failures and interface exceptions to application errors and system alerts. Traditional monitoring platforms typically notify operators only after issues have already affected business operations, resulting in lengthy investigations and repetitive manual troubleshooting.  This session presents a practical event-driven multi-agent architecture that transforms SAP incident management from reactive monitoring into proactive, AI-assisted operations. Built on SAP Business Technology Platform, the solution uses autonomous agents that activate only when operational events occur. Individual agents collaborate to detect incidents, classify severity, identify likely root causes, retrieve historical resolutions and operational knowledge, and deliver contextual recommendations directly into Microsoft Teams through a purpose-built integration.  The presentation focuses on architectural patterns rather than products, covering event-driven agent activation, multi-agent coordination, contextual retrieval, recommendation workflows, observability, and human approval checkpoints before remediation actions are executed. It also explains why a custom Teams integration was chosen instead of a generic assistant connector, enabling richer notification logic, tailored escalation workflows, and operational conversations designed specifically for incident response.  Attendees will gain practical insights into designing reliable AI agent systems, integrating them with enterprise operations, and balancing automation with governance. The session concludes with implementation lessons, operational trade-offs, and reusable architectural patterns that engineering teams can adopt to build intelligent, scalable, and trustworthy AI-powered operational platforms.
