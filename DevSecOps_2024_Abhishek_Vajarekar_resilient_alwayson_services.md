A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2024](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2024) by [Abhishek Vajarekar](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Abhishek_Vajarekar) (Software Development Manager). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2024_Abhishek_Vajarekar_resilient_alwayson_services

## Abstract

In a world where system failures are inevitable, resilience is key. This talk explores strategies like redundancy, failover, chaos engineering, and automated recovery to design systems that remain reliable under pressure. Learn how to turn failures into opportunities and ensure seamless user experiences, even in the face of disruption.
