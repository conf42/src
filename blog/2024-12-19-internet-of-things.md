# Where the physical world joins the internet

Conf42 Blog, 2024-12-19. https://www.conf42.com/blog/2024-12-19-internet-of-things

In short: And starts responding

Once upon a time, the internet lived safely inside computers.

Now it’s in cars, factories, medical devices, oil pipelines, kiosks, fridges, traffic systems, and that one sensor nobody remembers installing but everyone is afraid to touch. Welcome to IoT.

Conf42 Internet of Things 2024 is here to make sense of that world.

This conference covers the full IoT stack, from edge devices and connectivity to cloud platforms, analytics, security, and automation. You’ll find talks on robotics, industrial IoT, real-time analytics, digital twins, Kubernetes at the edge, and what happens when AI meets thousands of devices that never sleep.

AI is everywhere, obviously. Expect sessions on explainable AI for IoT, AI-driven cloud architectures, predictive analytics, automation, and using machine learning to turn massive streams of device data into something resembling a decision. There’s also plenty on observability, telemetry, and figuring out what’s actually going on when things stop behaving.

The use cases go wide. Healthcare, fintech, automotive, logistics, energy, retail, and smart infrastructure all show up. From contact tracing and crisis management to autonomous vehicles and supply chain optimization, this is IoT in the real world, not just diagrams on slides.

Security and reliability get their share of attention too. Identity federation, zero trust, blockchain, multi-layered defenses, and securing connected ecosystems where failure is not an option and “just reboot it” is rarely acceptable.

If your job involves devices, data, clouds, or explaining to someone why a sensor stopped reporting at 3 AM, this one’s for you. IoT is messy, powerful, and very much alive. Let’s talk about it.
