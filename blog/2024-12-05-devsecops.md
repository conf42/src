# When security stops being a checkpoint

Conf42 Blog, 2024-12-05. https://www.conf42.com/blog/2024-12-05-devsecops

In short: And becomes part of the workflow

DevSecOps is that thing everyone agrees is important and then quietly argues about in pull requests.

Should security shift left? Should it shift everywhere? Should it maybe stop breaking the build five minutes before a release? Big questions. Strong feelings.

Conf42 DevSecOps 2024 is here to unpack all of that.

This one goes wide and deep. There are talks on securing infrastructure, web applications, APIs, and Kubernetes clusters that absolutely did not ask to be exposed to the internet. You’ll hear about secrets management, Git leaks, identity federation, policy as code, and why credentials have a habit of showing up where they really shouldn’t.

AI is very much in the mix. Expect sessions on AIOps, alert noise reduction, AI-driven security assessments, explainable AI, and the slightly terrifying reality of AI being very interested in your secrets. There’s even a panel dedicated to that exact concern, because of course there is.

Beyond tools, the conference also digs into culture, governance, and the human side of security. Soft skills, leadership, compliance, resilience, and what DevSecOps looks like in healthcare, finance, supply chains, and other environments where mistakes are extra expensive.

You’ll find practical talks on Terraform, GitOps, cloud databases, internal developer platforms, and shifting from monoliths to microservices without everything catching fire. There’s also room for bigger conversations about trust, accountability, and what “secure by default” actually means in real teams.

If your job lives somewhere between shipping fast and sleeping at night, this one’s worth your time. DevSecOps isn’t getting simpler, but at least you don’t have to figure it out alone.
