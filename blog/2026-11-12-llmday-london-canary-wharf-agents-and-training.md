LLMday London takes over three screens at Everyman Canary Wharf on November 26, 2026, with more than thirty talks on training models, running agents and the bills that come with both. Speakers come from Cursive, MongoDB, UCL, Birkbeck, Whitney AI, NOFire AI, inDrive, The Trade Desk and GitLab, and the code **CONF42** gets you **42% off**.

The day runs alongside the Prompt Engineering Conference on the same Luma event, so prompt nerds and model trainers share the lobby. This post is about the LLMday half.

## From $1.50 to 3 trillion parameters

The training talks span the full budget range. **Gabriele Monti (Birkbeck, University of London)** asks *How far can $1.50 go?*: three projects with small open models, LoRA and cheap cloud GPUs, starting with résumé-to-JSON on Gemma 2B for about $1.50 of T4 time, and finding where that recipe stops working. At the other end, **Paulius Rauba (Cursive)** shares *Lessons learned from post-training 3 trillion parameter models*.

In between, **Nick Mecklenburg (Whitney AI)** explains *Searching Through a Virtual World: Post-Training a 4B Search Agent*, trained inside a synthetic company with employees, Slack threads, documents and calendars because real private data was off the table.

## Agents, in production, misbehaving

LLMday London does not pretend agents are easy.

- **Mark Weston (FireHorse)**, *AI Agents - what could possibly go wrong?*, opens with 7,000 emails sent to 50 people by his own agent, doing what it was allowed to do.
- **Artemis Leonardou (NOFire AI)**, *Three Hours Debugging Code I Never Wrote*: an agent's change passed review and CI, then took a production path down because the failure only existed in the combination of edits.
- **Nicholas Greenwood (aries global)**, *make the wrong thing hard: adapting codebases for agents*, on shaping the repo so the simplest path is the right one.
- **Sylvain Liege (GoWit-Hungary)** on stopping more than 70 collaborating LLM personas from inventing data.

## Living with it every day

**Anthony Bourached (UCL)** reports on *A Year Living in MindPalace*, an open-source personal agent that handles his email, teaching, experiments and a GPU cluster. **Edoardo Romani (MongoDB)** covers persona-based agents for GTM and RevOps teams, **Andreas Voniatis (Artios)** measures what moves brand visibility in *Reverse Engineering AI Search Results*, and **Kannupriya Kalra and Rory Graves** present *LLM4S 1.0: From Idea to Production AI in Scala/JVM*. Proof that the JVM crowd ships LLM apps too.

The keynotes are from **Coral** and **Miko Pawlikowski (Reliaburger)**, who brings *Ten years of Kubernetes lessons, in one binary*.
