# Conf42 SRE 2026 roundup: quiet failures, agentic ops, resilience

Conf42 Blog, 2026-03-20. https://www.conf42.com/blog/sre2026/sre-2026-roundup-quiet-failures-agentic-ops

In short: Conf42 Site Reliability Engineering 2026 in review, from HTTP/2 load balancing traps and in-place pod resize to MCP incident agents and resilience testing.

The best incident stories at [Conf42 Site Reliability Engineering 2026](https://www.conf42.com/sre2026) were the ones where nothing was technically down. One pod melting while its siblings idled, a security team slowed down by missing signals, a pricing cache that drifted. The event streamed online on March 19, 2026, with 35 talks from Harness, Virtana, Spotify, Rootly, Bloomberg, gridX, Walmart, Oracle, Microsoft, JPMorgan Chase, Wells Fargo and others, and it balanced those field stories against a strong thread on letting AI agents into operations.

## Failures that don't show up on the dashboard

[Mariem Sboui](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Mariem_Sboui_kubernetes_default_load) (gridX) told the kind of story that saves other teams a week. After HTTP/2 traffic arrived in their Kubernetes cluster, one backend pod ran hot on CPU and latency while the others sat nearly idle, and node metrics said there was plenty of capacity. The cause: Kubernetes Services and kube-proxy balance at Layer 4, per connection, and HTTP/2 multiplexes many requests over one long-lived connection. If you've ever wondered why your gRPC service has a favourite pod, watch this one.

[Abhimanyu Narwal](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Abhimanyu_Narwal_failures_dashboards_reliability) (Bloomberg) presented a case study from security operations where systems stayed up, yet missing signals and reduced clarity slowed people's diagnosis and response without triggering a single alarm. [Stuart Clark](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Stuart_Clark_scale_fail_spotify) (Spotify) went back to Spotify's 2014 hyper-growth, when abstractions oversimplified the system and hurt incident response; the lesson was that a good abstraction teaches complexity instead of hiding it. [Khushboo Nigam](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Khushboo_Nigam_correlation_over_collection) (Oracle) made the same argument for telemetry: SREs have a clarity problem, not a data problem, so organise signals by decision value and make "what changed?" the easy question.

## Agents in the incident channel, with guardrails

Several speakers put LLMs to work on diagnosis. [Makarand Gujarathi](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Makarand_Gujarathi_assisted_incident_response) (Walmart) started from a familiar cost: engineers spending two to four hours and writing 15 to 30 SQL queries to correlate logs, metrics and traces across 5 to 10 backend components with skewed timestamps. His approach uses LLMs and MCP to do the correlation. [David McNerney and Daniel Raskin](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_David_McNerney_Daniel_Raskin_root_cause_distributed_systems) (Virtana) argued that outages are system problems, not application problems, and demoed agents investigating incidents over an automatically discovered dependency graph spanning services, Kubernetes, network and storage. [Savi Grover](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Savi_Grover_alerts_answers_modern) (Tekskills) and [Rohit Wadhwa](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Rohit_Wadhwa_predictive_workflow_integrity) (Walmart) covered agentic ops and autonomous triage across regions.

The counterweight came from talks about limits. [Ajay Athitya Ramanathan](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Ajay_Athitya_Ramanathan_autonomy_guardrails_operating) (FourthSquare) noted that agentic failures in finance and operations rarely look like model errors; they show up as exception backlogs, repeated overrides and escalations. [Suganya Nagarajan](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Suganya_Nagarajan_human_governed_automation) (Amazon) described human-governed automation loops for systems where autonomy makes failures spread faster, and [Vishal Shah](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Vishal_Shah_reliable_support_human) (Visa) kept a human in the loop of an agentic RAG system for API support.

[Sylvain Kalache](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Sylvain_Kalache_vibes_outages_writes) (Rootly) took the other side of AI in production: code written by assistants, tests that mirror the same flawed logic, hallucinated dependencies, more churn and bigger batches, landing on leaner SRE teams whose developers know their own code less well.

## Resilience you test, not resilience you hope for

[Uma Mukkara](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Uma_Mukkara_resilience_testing_enterprise), who leads resilience testing at Harness and co-created LitmusChaos, gave the keynote: outages are inevitable, untested resilience accumulates like technical debt, and chaos, load and disaster recovery testing work better as one practice run with every delivery than as three teams' separate projects.

[Bhargavaram Potharaju](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Bhargavaram_Potharaju_national_scale_regulatory) (Wells Fargo) covered HA, DR and audit-ready design for national-scale regulatory reporting, [Rajesh Kumar Balusu](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Rajesh_Kumar_Balusu_building_highly_available) (AGAP Technologies) highly available databases, and [Shruthi Sepuri](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Shruthi_Sepuri_testing_reliability_strengthening) (TCS) the gap between functional testing and reliability in regulated systems.

## Platform mechanics: CPU, VMs, caches and queues

[Raman Tehlan](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Raman_Tehlan_Shubham_Rai_unlocking_just_time) (Zurich Lab) and Shubham Rai (Truefoundry) combined Kubernetes in-place pod resize with PSI (Pressure Stall Information) metrics to adjust CPU requests without restarts, aimed at clusters that are over-provisioned and throttled at the same time. [Shruthi Rajashekar](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Shruthi_Rajashekar_reducing_call_pain) (Broadcom) made VMs first-class Kubernetes resources to cut on-call pain on hybrid platforms. [Mukul Kumar Gaur](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Mukul_Kumar_Gaur_ready_shopping_caching) (Accelya) explained how airline NDC shopping platforms cache offers for metasearch traffic without letting prices drift. [Ran Tao](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Ran_Tao_migration_prem_messaging) (AWS) walked through rehosting, replatforming and refactoring messaging systems on the way to the cloud, and [Abhishek Suman](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Abhishek_Suman_compute_sharded_stream) (Microsoft) compute-sharded stream processing for petabyte-scale security analytics.

## SRE outside the server room

The event also showed how far SRE practice now travels. Talks applied it to clinical handoffs ([Abhiram Potharaju](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Abhiram_Potharaju_reliable_clinical_handoffs), Wells Fargo), healthcare utilisation management letters ([Prakash Easwaran](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Prakash_Easwaran_reliable_driven_letters), ZeOmega), bioreactor analytics in biologics labs ([Amogha Tenneti](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Amogha_Tenneti_driven_automation_flex), Eurofins PSS), grocery retail LLMs ([Sanjay Basu](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Sanjay_Basu_operationalizing_llms_scale), TCS) and frontend architecture ([Murali Varma](https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2026_Murali_Varma_driven_frontend_architecture), Galileo Financial Technologies).
