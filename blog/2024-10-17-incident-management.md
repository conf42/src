# Red alerts happen

Conf42 Blog, 2024-10-17. https://www.conf42.com/blog/2024-10-17-incident-management

In short: Good crews bring everyone home

Let’s be honest. Nobody wakes up excited about incident management.

You don’t pour your morning coffee thinking, “Ah yes, today I would love a surprise outage, a flood of alerts, and a Slack channel named incident-sev-1-final-final-v3.” And yet… here we are.

The good news is, you don’t have to suffer alone.

Conf42 Incident Management 2024 is bringing together a whole lineup of people who spend an impressive amount of time dealing with things going sideways. On purpose. There are talks on AI, automation, cloud, security, data, monitoring, and all the fun stuff that usually shows up right when you were about to log off.

You’ll hear about AIOps, SRE tooling, chatbots that might actually be helpful, debugging ML systems, handling vendor incidents, and figuring out why the network decided to have feelings today. There’s also plenty on architecture, resilience, zero trust, and reducing false positives, because nothing builds character like being paged for something that wasn’t broken.

Some sessions dive into regulated worlds like healthcare, finance, and utilities, where incidents are extra spicy and “just restart it” is not a valid strategy. Others focus on cloud migrations, Kubernetes security, and the quiet art of keeping systems upright while everyone else is asking what went wrong.

If incidents are part of your job (or your life, at this point), this one’s for you. Bring your curiosity, your scars, and maybe a sense of humor. You’ll probably need it.

See you there!
