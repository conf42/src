# SREday Chennai is free, two tracks, at Hexaware HQ on December 5

Conf42 Blog, 2026-11-21. https://www.conf42.com/blog/2026-11-21-sreday-chennai-free-at-hexaware

In short: SREday Chennai runs December 5, 2026 at Hexaware HQ in Siruseri - a free, two-track day on SRE, DevOps and cloud. Apply on Luma to get a seat.

SREday Chennai happens on December 5, 2026, at Hexaware HQ in Siruseri, Chennai: a one-day, two-track conference on site reliability engineering, DevOps and cloud. It is free to attend, and seats go to people who apply on Luma and are approved.

If you know SREday from London, Amsterdam or the other cities, the format is the same: practitioners on stage, breaks built into the schedule, and a lobby where the real arguments about alert fatigue happen.

## What the day looks like

The plan on the event page is a single day with two rooms running in parallel, a coffee break before the talks, lunch and networking after the first block, and a networking and sponsor crawl later in the afternoon. The day starts at 09:30 India time. The event page lists 20+ speakers across the two tracks.

Hexaware hosts the day, with Harness among the sponsors.

## The lineup

The speaker list is still being put together, and the schedule appears on the [SREday Chennai page](https://sreday.com/2026-chennai-q4) once it is published. We will not guess at names here. What we can say is what SREday programmes tend to cover, because the brand has a clear scope: incident response and postmortems, observability, Kubernetes and platform engineering, cloud cost, and, increasingly, what happens when AI agents start touching production systems.

If you would rather be on stage than in the audience, the call for papers sits at [cfp.ninja](https://cfp.ninja/e/sreday-2026-chennai-q4). The talks that land best at SREday are real stories: the outage, the migration, the runbook that lied.

## Why a Conf42 viewer should go

Conf42 runs online, which is great for watching a talk at 2x speed and terrible for asking the speaker a follow-up question. SREday is the in-person half. If [Conf42 Site Reliability Engineering 2026](https://www.conf42.com/sre2026) or [Conf42 DevSecOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2026) is in your watch history, Chennai is the room where those conversations continue, with a lunch break to keep arguing through.

It is also a rare free conference at a company campus, which makes it an easy sell to your manager: no ticket, no expense report, one day.

## How to get in

Registration is free on [Luma](https://luma.com/event/evt-qaCxAaz03W6wVuP). Fill in the application and wait for the approval; since seats are approved one by one, apply sooner rather than later. Updates on speakers and the schedule land on the [event page](https://sreday.com/2026-chennai-q4) first.
