# Conf42 Observability 2025 roundup: instrument, tame OTel, cut costs

Conf42 Blog, 2025-06-06. https://www.conf42.com/blog/obs2025/observability-2025-roundup-instrumentation-otel-bills

In short: Conf42 Observability 2025 in review, from config-driven instrumentation and eBPF to honest OpenTelemetry lessons, smaller bills, Kafka signals and identity.

Auto-instrumentation gets you about 80% of the way; the last 20% is manual work, and at Wave, Jean-Mark Wright estimated it at roughly ten years of it. That is where his talk title, "Instrumenting at 10 years per second", comes from, and it set up the main tension of [Conf42 Observability 2025](https://www.conf42.com/obs2025): teams want deeper telemetry, and they want to spend less effort and less money getting it. With 61 talks streamed online on June 5, 2025, it was one of the largest Conf42 events of the year, with speakers from Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Meta, Salesforce, Confluent, Adobe, WSO2, Palo Alto Networks, AT&T and Telstra.

## Better instrumentation for less effort

[Jean-Mark Wright](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_JeanMark_Wright_instrumenting_second_months) (Wave) opened with an on-call story: the system was quietly giving a premium feature, bank transaction import, to customers who hadn't paid for it, and only detailed manual instrumentation made the cause findable. His team's answer is Config-Accelerated Custom Instrumentation, which captures telemetry from function inputs through configuration instead of hand-written spans.

[Isala Piyarisi and Nilushan Costa](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Isala_Piyarisi_Nilushan_Costa_fluent_opensearch_zero) (WSO2) went further and rebuilt their platform's observability on Fluent Bit, OpenSearch and Prometheus with zero-instrumentation telemetry via eBPF, reporting log latency under five seconds and large cost savings. If you want to write the eBPF yourself, [Sooter Saalu](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Sooter_Saalu_writing_programs_ebpf) (Draft.dev) covered custom programs for tracing syscalls and profiling latency. [Divya Darshana](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Divya_Darshana_clarity_gofr_grpc) showed how the open source Go framework GoFr builds tracing, logging and metrics into gRPC services, and [Dharmendra Ahuja](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Dharmendra_Ahuja_fluent_eks_aws) walked through Fluent Bit on EKS.

## OpenTelemetry, with the caveats left in

Two talks gave OpenTelemetry an honest review. [Narendra Reddy Sanikommu](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Narendra_Reddy_Sanikommu_opentelemetry_messy_problems) (NVIDIA) credited it with removing vendor lock-in and unifying OpenTracing and OpenCensus into one standard across languages, then spent the rest of the talk on the new problems it creates and how to tame them. [Meletius Mgbeodichimma Igbokwe](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Meletius_Mgbeodichimma_Igbokwe_lessons_field_bullet) collected field lessons from teams that tried to make it work in production. Between them you get a realistic adoption plan, not a brochure. [Vignesh Iyer](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Vignesh_Iyer_lightweight_teams_system) (NVIDIA) added an open source pipeline for lean teams, and [Neel Shah](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Neel_Shah_logs_metrics_traces) (Middleware) looked at what comes after the three pillars.

## The bill is an observability signal too

Cost had its own track in all but name. [Aritra Ghosh](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Aritra_Ghosh_shrinking_kubernetes_cost) (Microsoft) shared techniques to cut Kubernetes monitoring spend without losing visibility, and [Venkata Madhu Prateek Reddy Kambala](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Venkata_Madhu_Prateek_Reddy_Kambala_logging_cost_cutting) (Synapsis) traced rising logging bills to their root causes. [Yury Nino](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Yury_Nino_series_cost_anomalies) (Google) turned the problem around, running time series analysis on logs to catch billing anomalies before the invoice does. [Denish Tomar](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Denish_Tomar_dashboards_clouds_fun) (Qumulus) argued for fixed-cost infrastructure so dashboards stop being a monthly surprise, and [Ganeshkumar Palanisamy](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Ganeshkumar_Palanisamy_platforms_scale_finops) (Reltio) combined AIOps with FinOps on multi-cloud data platforms.

## Find the signals that matter in your system

The most reusable talks picked one kind of system and asked which signals actually matter. [Abhishek Walia](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Abhishek_Walia_kafka_visibility_scale) (Confluent) pointed out that Kafka doesn't fail cleanly; it stalls and lags, so you need the few signals that catch that early. [Vamsi Praveen Karanam](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Vamsi_Praveen_Karanam_aws_serverless_workflows) (Amazon) tackled Step Functions fan-outs that launch thousands of Lambdas, streaming telemetry into a graph neural network to spot hot branches. [Sudeep Kumar](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Sudeep_Kumar_traces_user_synthetic) (Salesforce) aggregated synthetic traces into critical user journeys, and [Palak Bhawsar](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Palak_Bhawsar_synthetic_monitoring_outage) (Telstra) made the case for synthetic monitoring that catches the outage while you sleep.

Data and frontend teams got their turn. [Chaitanya Reddy Krishnama](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Chaitanya_Reddy_Krishnama_dashboards_qa_nightmares) (MoneyGram) described dashboards that looked great until they started lying because of silent pipeline failures and schema drift. [Vadim Tsaregorodtsev](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Vadim_Tsaregorodtsev_passport_frontend_navigating) (ETG) gave every frontend project an Observability Passport linking its logs, Sentry and security tools. [Alexandru Hang](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Alexandru_Hang_logs_behaviour_experience) (ING Tech Romania) used activity logs to predict what users will do next and prefetch assets. [Ashwin Das Gururaja and Devang Gaur](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Ashwin_Das_Gururaja_Devang_Gaur_dashboard_payment_commerce) (Adobe) covered end-to-end observability for checkout and payments.

## Identity and security belong in the telemetry

[Cynthia Akiotu](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Cynthia_Akiotu_identity_access_breach) (Tesco Bank) argued that most observability ignores who is doing what, and that privilege misuse and insider threats only show up when identity is part of the picture. [Srikanth Potla](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Srikanth_Potla_modeling_insight_spots) (SoFi) threat-modelled the observability stack itself, since logs, metrics and traces leak too. [Krishna Ganeriwal](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Krishna_Ganeriwal_privacy_infrastructure) (Meta) looked at observability for privacy infrastructure, [Chintan Udeshi](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Chintan_Udeshi_edge_realtime_security) (Palo Alto Networks) at edge security monitoring, and [Onyebuchi Mark Irozuru](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Onyebuchi_Mark_Irozuru_signatures_observability_federated) (Botanixlabs) at monitoring federated multisig node operators with Grafana Alloy.

## Further afield

Observability ideas also travelled: AI-assisted pipelines to reduce noise and MTTR at Informatica ([Kirti Ranjan Parida and Sarav Jagadeesan](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Kirti_Ranjan_Parida_Sarav_Jagadeesan_mttr_pipelines_reduce)), generative models on Amazon Bedrock ([Hazel Andrea Saenz Giron](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Hazel_Andrea_Saenz_Giron_serverless_bedrock_generative), Caylent), aircraft digital twins ([Divakar Duraiyan](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Divakar_Duraiyan_aircraft_digital_twins), TCS), and event-driven collaboration between electronic and mechanical hardware design teams ([Pradeep Karanam](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Pradeep_Karanam_ecad_mcad)). For something different, [Anastasiia Zvenigorodskaia](https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Anastasiia_Zvenigorodskaia_opensource_profitable_making) (Epicmax) talked about making open source profitable.
