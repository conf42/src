# Teaching machines by example and constraint

Conf42 Blog, 2024-11-14. https://www.conf42.com/blog/2024-11-14-prompt-engineering

In short: Language does the work

At some point this year, a lot of us realized we’re no longer just writing code.

We’re negotiating. With models. Politely. Repeatedly. Sometimes aggressively. Sometimes with emojis. Sometimes with “no, seriously, do it like this”.

Welcome to prompt engineering.

Conf42 Prompt Engineering 2024 is all about that strange new layer between humans and machines, where wording matters, context is everything, and a single sentence can be the difference between brilliance and absolute nonsense.

There are talks on securing prompts, preventing injection attacks, and making sure your AI doesn’t spill secrets the moment you look away. Others dig into orchestration, agents completing missions, and turning prompts into actual systems that do useful work instead of just vibing.

You’ll also find plenty of real-world use cases. Healthcare. Retail. Finance. HR. Education. Cloud platforms. Testing. Even SRE perspectives on keeping AI-driven systems reliable when prompts start getting creative at 2 AM.

Some sessions go deep into LLMs, multimodal models, embeddings, vectors, and feedback loops. Others take a more human approach, exploring ethics, bias, hallucinations, and what it means to trust AI output when the stakes are higher than a funny chatbot reply.

If you’ve ever thought “why did the model do that” or “how do I get this thing to behave”, this conference is probably for you. And if prompt engineering has quietly become part of your job title, well… you’re definitely in the right place.
