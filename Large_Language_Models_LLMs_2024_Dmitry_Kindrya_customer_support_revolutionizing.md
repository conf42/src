## Abstract

Join me to explore whether these models are the game-changers or potential disruptors, and gain insights into navigating this transformative landscape.

## Key takeaways

- Large language models are AI driven systems capable of generating human like text. LLM models have significantly transformed customer support operations. While proponents argue they revolutionize support by offering instant and accurate responses, detractors express concerns about their potential to undermine traditional methods.

-  hybrid approaches represent a fusion of the capabilities of LLM models with the indispensable human touch in customer support. These strategies aim to harness the strengths of both automation and human interaction to deliver exceptional customer experience. Businesses must adapt and evolve their support strategies to stay relevant and competitive.

- How do stakeholders perceive the role of LLM models in customer support? Customers expect faster response times and personalized interactions. Many customers appreciate the convenience of automated support but value human intervention. Industry experts they foresee continued advancements but stress the need for ethical considerations and adaptation.

- Are LLM models truly revolutionizing customer support, or do they risk undermining the essential elements of human connection and trust? Finding the balance between automation and human interaction will be crucial in shaping the future of customer support.
