# Many Layers of Availability

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/platform2024) by [Ilya Kaznacheev](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Ilya_Kaznacheev) (Lead Cloud and Applications Architect @ Capgemini). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2024_Ilya_Kaznacheev_availability_layers

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gK0cY-zw3lI

## Abstract

Discover the true path to building reliable systems beyond microservices. Join my talk to explore the interconnected factors of availability, from infrastructure to architecture. You’ll leave with a comprehensive roadmap for achieving high availability in advanced cloud-native systems.
