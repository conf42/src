# From Demo to Deployment: Building, Evaluating, and Observing Multimodal Conversational Agents

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Gobu Natarajan](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Gobu_Natarajan) (Manager - Technical Program Management @ Amazon). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Gobu_Natarajan_multimodal_agents_conversation

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QmwTwUS-tDo

## Abstract

Multimodal AI is leaving the demo stage. Learn how production ready voice, vision, and touch agents deliver 37% higher task success, and how DevOps teams design, deploy, observe, and scale these systems with real world architectures and metrics.
