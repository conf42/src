How to build your own green observability pipeline

Video size:

Abstract

As a developer, staying curious is the most important trait. Next is getting motivated to build your own projects. In this talk, I guide you throughout my journey of setting up a green observability pipeline, built entirely from open source projects. I’ll explain to the audience how to start contributing to open source projects, communicate with maintainers, overcome technical hurdles and complete the mission. A technical talk referenced by a blog post and a GitHub repository.
