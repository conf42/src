    Abstract

    AI can help you build a side project quickly. The harder question is whether you can operate it without becoming its permanent support team. Drawing from my experiments building and running multiple projects alongside a full-time job, I’ll describe the problem as a platform-engineering problem at a team size of one: reducing repeated work, making delegation usable, and deciding which tasks deserve human attention. I’ll share what worked and what failed when I used AI beyond coding, automated routine work, and built tools and controls around the projects. The talk focuses on the operating model after launch, with practical takeaways for developers who want more independent projects without more obligations.
