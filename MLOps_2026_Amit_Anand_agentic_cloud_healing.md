## Abstract

Agentic AI is reshaping how cloud infrastructure is designed, deployed, and operated. As autonomous systems increasingly perceive, reason, and act, traditional cloud architectures face challenges in managing unpredictable workloads, low-latency requirements, and dynamic resource demands. This session explores practical architectural approaches to building scalable, reliable, and adaptive infrastructure for agentic AI through MLOps principles.

The talk examines how Kubernetes, multi-cloud environments, and orchestration frameworks such as LangChain can support agentic AI workloads. It highlights architectural considerations for resource scaling, workload orchestration, infrastructure automation, and operational efficiency.

A key focus is self-healing cloud operations, where collaborative AI agents can assist with incident detection, automated remediation, and system recovery. The session also explores observability and automation maturity gaps that affect reliability, performance, and service-level objectives.

Attendees will gain insights into designing agentic AI infrastructure with security, governance, compliance, and cost optimization in mind, along with practical considerations for integrating autonomous operations into existing cloud environments.
