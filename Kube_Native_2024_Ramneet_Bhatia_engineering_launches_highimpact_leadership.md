# Leading High-Impact Engineering Launches

A talk at [Conf42 Kube Native 2024](https://www.conf42.com/kubenative2024) by [Ramneet Bhatia](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Ramneet_Bhatia) (Senior Software Engineer @ Netflix). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Kube_Native_2024_Ramneet_Bhatia_engineering_launches_highimpact_leadership

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0CatCDwWmbk

## Abstract

In this talk, I outline my learnings on leading critical and high impact engineering  launches based on my experience captaining the launch of paid sharing at Netflix.
