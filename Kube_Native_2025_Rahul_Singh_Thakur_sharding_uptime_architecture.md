# Kube-Native Sharding: Multi-Zone Service Architecture for 99.99% Uptime

A talk at [Conf42 Kube Native 2025](https://www.conf42.com/kubenative2025) by [Rahul Singh Thakur](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Rahul_Singh_Thakur) (Lead Member of Technical Staff @ Salesforce). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Kube_Native_2025_Rahul_Singh_Thakur_sharding_uptime_architecture

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j5un96J72ME

## Abstract

Practical guide to implementing multi-zone sharded microservices architecture achieving 99.99% uptime. Covers real-world Kubernetes deployment patterns, intelligent request routing, and operational strategies for fault-tolerant cloud-native systems.
