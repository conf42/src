# Scaling Microservices Observability with Agentic LLMs

A talk at [Conf42 MLOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/mlops2026) by [Nishanth Vepachedu](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Nishanth_Vepachedu) (NIT Warangal, India @ stealth). Page: https://www.conf42.com/MLOps_2026_Nishanth_Vepachedu_microservices_observability_llms

## Abstract

Modern microservices generate massive volumes of logs, metrics, and distributed traces, making incident detection, root cause analysis, and service reliability increasingly challenging. Traditional observability tools often struggle to correlate signals across complex service dependencies, resulting in delayed investigations and operational blind spots.  This session explores how Agentic Large Language Models (LLMs) can enhance cloud-native observability through semantic analysis, intelligent telemetry correlation, automated causal reasoning, and closed-loop remediation.  Focusing on architecture and operational practices, the presentation examines approaches to integrating LLM-driven analysis into existing observability workflows. Drawing on published research and industry findings, it discusses improvements in event correlation, service dependency mapping, alert accuracy, and incident response, including reductions in Mean Time to Detection (MTTD) and Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR).  Attendees will gain insights into scalability, performance optimization, debugging, security, data privacy, and governance considerations. The session highlights practical approaches to improving observability, automating incident response, and maintaining resilient distributed systems at scale.
