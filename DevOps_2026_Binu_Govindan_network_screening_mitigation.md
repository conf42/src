# AI-Driven Threat Screening for 5G UPF with Automated Risk Mitigation

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Binu Govindan](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Binu_Govindan) (5G -Azure -Prinicipal Engineer @ Principal SDET at Microsoft). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Binu_Govindan_network_screening_mitigation

## Abstract

Discover how AI-driven, multi-factor risk scoring and automated mitigation transform 5G UPF security. Learn how real-time behavioral detection and programmable defenses cut alert fatigue, speed incident response, and harden networks against emerging threats.
