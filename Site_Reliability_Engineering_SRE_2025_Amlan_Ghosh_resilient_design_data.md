# Resilient by Design: Data-Driven Migration from Monoliths to Event-Driven Microservices

A talk at [Conf42 Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) 2025](https://www.conf42.com/sre2025) by [Amlan Ghosh](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Amlan_Ghosh) (Biju Patnaik University of Technology). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2025_Amlan_Ghosh_resilient_design_data

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3lE2cpiBeDM

## Abstract

Discover how to transform monolithic systems into resilient event-driven microservices without sacrificing stability. Learn battle-tested patterns from Fortune 500 case studies that reduce complexity, speed deployments, and enhance recovery times.
