## Abstract

Explore the game-changing transition from FinFET to Gate-All-Around (GAA) technology! Discover how GAA unlocks transformative gains in power, performance, and chip density, revolutionizing AI, IoT, and HPC. Dive into cutting-edge EDA tools, AI-driven workflows
