# The Road Ahead: Istio Observability

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/platform2024) by [Siddhartha Khare](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Siddhartha_Khare) (Senior Technical Account Manager @ New Relic). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2024_Siddhartha_Khare_istio_observability_road

## Abstract

Istio: A Game-Changer for Microservices. In today's fast-paced tech world, effective microservices management is essential. This talk dives deep into Istio Observability (O11y), showcasing how it accelerates development, enhances quality, and drives market competitiveness.
