# AI Driven Vectorized Motor Health Monitoring for Reliable Industrial Operations

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Avinash Sista](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Avinash_Sista) (Director/Principal @ MongoDB). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Avinash_Sista_maintenance_industrial_ai

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yb6CF76VLKk

## Abstract

AI meets industrial reliability. Learn how vectorized motor health data turns raw sensor signals into predictive insights, enabling early fault detection, smarter maintenance, and higher uptime through DevOps aligned observability and AI driven analytics.
