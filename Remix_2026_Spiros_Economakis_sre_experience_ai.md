# How AI is redefining SRE and Customer Experience

A talk at [Conf42 Remix 2026](https://www.conf42.com/remix2026) by [Spiros Economakis](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Spiros_Economakis) (Founder & CEO @ NOFire AI). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Remix_2026_Spiros_Economakis_sre_experience_ai

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bXyvcbcW7WY

## Abstract

AI is reshaping SRE, but the real opportunity isn’t faster RCA, it’s building proactive, customer-centered reliability. This talk explores how AI and culture together move us from firefighting to foresight.
