# Design Tokens as Platform Infrastructure: Architecting Scalable Multi-Platform Design Systems

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/platform2025) by [Pallav Laskar](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Pallav_Laskar) (Principal Software Engineer @ Zscaler). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2025_Pallav_Laskar_design_tokens_multi

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GoOk5RQk1aA

## Abstract

Turn design chaos into automated infrastructure! See how Zscaler cut 240kB bundles & boosted performance 18% by treating design tokens like code. Live demos of GitOps workflows, automated theming, and zero-runtime overhead magic that scales across platforms.
