    Abstract

    Prompt quality is only one part of building reliable AI systems. In enterprise environments, the quality of retrieved context, the freshness of knowledge, and the ability to govern information often have a greater impact on model responses than prompt wording alone. As Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) becomes the foundation for enterprise AI, designing effective retrieval pipelines has become essential for improving accuracy and consistency. This session presents a practical five-axis framework for enterprise RAG systems covering ingestion, indexing, retrieval composition, grounding strategies, and governance. Based on a comparative study of approximately 25 academic, open source, and commercial implementations, including eight major enterprise platforms, the session examines how production systems differ from research prototypes and why capabilities such as ACL-aware retrieval, provenance tracking, and time-aware evaluation are increasingly important for trustworthy AI. Attendees will also explore seven engineering challenges that continue to shape enterprise AI, including long-document grounding, retrieval across heterogeneous content, freshness through change data capture, confidentiality-preserving evaluation, multi-agent retrieval, and balancing latency with operational cost. Rather than focusing on prompt templates or a specific framework, the session provides a vendor-neutral perspective on designing knowledge systems that consistently deliver higher-quality context for enterprise AI applications.
