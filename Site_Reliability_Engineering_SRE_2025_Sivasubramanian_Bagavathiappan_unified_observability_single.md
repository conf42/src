# Unified Observability-A Single Pane of Glass and Single Source of truth

A talk at [Conf42 Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) 2025](https://www.conf42.com/sre2025) by [Sivasubramanian Bagavathiappan](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sivasubramanian_Bagavathiappan) (SRE Leader @ GuhaTek). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Site_Reliability_Engineering_SRE_2025_Sivasubramanian_Bagavathiappan_unified_observability_single

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ua9TWPnVsGE

## Abstract

Managing multiple monitoring tools in an organization can lead to fragmented insights and delayed decision-making. Unified observability provides a solution by integrating diverse data sources into one, enabling teams to quickly identify issues, perform RCA, and take proactive actions.
