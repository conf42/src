# Threat Modeling in DevOps: A Practical Guide for Developers

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/devops2025) by [Krishna Chaitanya Rudraraju](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Krishna_Chaitanya_Rudraraju) (Senior Software Engineer @ Microsoft). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2025_Krishna_Chaitanya_Rudraraju_threat_modeling_developers

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=am7Ca6YrFko

## Abstract

Shift security left with threat modeling! Learn how to identify, mitigate, and validate risks in your DevOps pipelines without slowing down delivery. This practical guide for developers will arm you with tools, real-world examples, and actionable steps to build secure software at DevOps speed!
