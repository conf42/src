# MLOps for AI-Driven Municipal Bond Intelligence and Execution

A talk at [Conf42 MLOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/mlops2026) by [Tenny Enoch Devadas](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Tenny_Enoch_Devadas) (Principal Engineer @ Fidelity). Page: https://www.conf42.com/MLOps_2026_Tenny_Enoch_Devadas_municipal_bond_intelligence

## Abstract

Municipal bonds finance essential U.S. infrastructure, but their lifecycle produces large volumes of documents, disclosures, financial data, market information, and trading inputs across municipalities, advisors, underwriters, investors, and trading desks. This session presents a practical architecture for applying MLOps principles to connect these information flows and continuously operate AI-driven municipal bond intelligence.  The workflow begins with an AI Document Intelligence Assistant that processes official statements, financial reports, continuing disclosures, ratings, and security information from sources such as EMMA/MSRB and Bloomberg. It extracts attributes including CUSIP, coupon, maturity, call provisions, revenues, outstanding debt, debt-service coverage, pension obligations, and covenants. Validation against prior filings and reference data supports credit and anomaly detection for identifying material changes, disclosure inconsistencies, financial deterioration, or improving credit conditions.  The resulting intelligence feeds a Portfolio Manager AI Assistant that evaluates credit quality alongside yield curves, relative value, duration, liquidity, ratings, portfolio exposure, investment guidelines, and state and sector concentration. The system presents Buy, Hold, Sell, or Review recommendations while keeping the portfolio manager as the final decision-maker.  After approval, a Trader AI Assistant evaluates fair value and liquidity, supports dealer and RFQ strategy, analyzes quotes, and helps document explainable best-execution decisions. The architecture then extends into a six-stage operational feedback loop: Document AI → Credit Intelligence → Portfolio Manager AI → Trader AI → Post-Trade AI → Continuous Monitoring.  The session focuses on practical architecture, automation, validation, monitoring, explainability, and continuous feedback, showing how MLOps practices can help operate AI workflows reliably across the municipal bond lifecycle.
