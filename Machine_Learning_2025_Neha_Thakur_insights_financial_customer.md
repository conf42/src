# AI-Powered Customer Insights for Financial Services

A talk at [Conf42 Machine Learning 2025](https://www.conf42.com/ml2025) by [Neha Thakur](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Neha_Thakur) (Associate Solutions Architect @ AWS). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Machine_Learning_2025_Neha_Thakur_insights_financial_customer

## Abstract

Unlock the power of your customer interactions with AWS AI services. Learn how to transform raw conversation data into actionable insights, enabling financial institutions to enhance customer satisfaction, identify emerging trends, and streamline operations across multiple communication channels.
