## Abstract

Discover how Agentic AI transforms brittle enterprise integrations into self-healing systems. Learn how AI-orchestrated networks detect failures before they cascade, slashing recovery times while maintaining near-perfect uptime. The future of resilient integration is here—and it's autonomous.
