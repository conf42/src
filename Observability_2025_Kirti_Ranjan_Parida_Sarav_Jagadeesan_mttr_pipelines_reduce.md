# Next-Gen Observability: Leveraging AI and Data Pipelines to reduce Cost and MTTR

A talk at [Conf42 Observability 2025](https://www.conf42.com/obs2025) by [Kirti Ranjan Parida](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Kirti_Ranjan_Parida) (DevOps Architect @ Informatica) and [Sarav Jagadeesan](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sarav_Jagadeesan) (Director of Platform Engineering & Infrastructure @ Informatica). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Kirti_Ranjan_Parida_Sarav_Jagadeesan_mttr_pipelines_reduce

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y4hSuAEs5uo

## Abstract

Next-generation observability pipelines, powered by AI, are transforming how organizations manage complex systems by dramatically reducing both operational costs and MTTR. They filter out noise and self heal system with less human involved in incident management
