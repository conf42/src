# Data-Driven Incident Management: Streamlining Content Migration from Salesforce to AEM Guides

A talk at [Conf42 Incident Management 2024](https://www.conf42.com/im2024) by [Bhanu Gogula](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Bhanu_Gogula) (University of Central Missouri). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Incident_Management_2024_Bhanu_Gogula_11_data_salesforce_aem

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kaVbTudUtT4

## Abstract

Discover how to seamlessly migrate content from Salesforce to AEM Guides while minimizing downtime and ensuring data integrity! In this talk, we'll share practical tips, code snippets, and automation hacks that streamline the process, reduce incidents, and keep your platform running smoothly.
