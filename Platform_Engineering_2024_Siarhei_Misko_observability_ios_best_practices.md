# Building Observability into Your iOS App Platform: Tools, Techniques, and Best Practices

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/platform2024) by [Siarhei Misko](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Siarhei_Misko) (iOS Team Lead @ Rakuten Viber). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2024_Siarhei_Misko_observability_ios_best_practices

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ff20DuMctQ

## Abstract

Dive into the essential world of Observability and discover the pivotal differences from Monitoring. From development analytics to the art of log management, learn how to collect, store, and analyze data like a pro.
