# SRE for Supply Chain: Building Reliable Human-AI Warehouse Operations at Scale

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Vishnu Prasad](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Vishnu_Prasad) (Software Developer @ ERP Initiatives Group Inc). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Vishnu_Prasad_supplychain_reliability_warehouses

## Abstract

Supply chain systems are production systems. Discover how SRE principles, observability patterns, and platform engineering create predictable, self-healing warehouse operations where AI and human workers collaborate effectively at scale.
