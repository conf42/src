# Culture is Still a Challenge

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/devops2025) by [Mandi Walls](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Mandi_Walls) (DevOps Advocate @ PagerDuty). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2025_Mandi_Walls_culture_challenges_devops

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M0F_oBRWFJ4

## Abstract

When the only tool you have is a hammer, every problem looks like a nail. For technical teams, their control over their environment is often limited to the technical tools they choose to use to implement their projects.
