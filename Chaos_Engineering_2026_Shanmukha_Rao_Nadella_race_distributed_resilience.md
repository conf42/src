# Engineering Race-Condition Resilience in Distributed Systems

A talk at [Conf42 Chaos Engineering 2026](https://www.conf42.com/chaos2026) by [Shanmukha Rao Nadella](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Shanmukha_Rao_Nadella) (Lead Application Development Engineer @ American Family Insurance). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Chaos_Engineering_2026_Shanmukha_Rao_Nadella_race_distributed_resilience

## Abstract

Race conditions are a critical reliability challenge in distributed systems, where concurrent transactions, delayed events, and unpredictable execution sequences can produce inconsistent data, duplicate updates, lost transactions, and operational failures. These problems become particularly difficult to diagnose and recover from in cloud-native, event-driven architectures.  This session presents a practical architectural framework for reducing race conditions and improving system resilience under concurrent workloads and failure scenarios. The approach combines five capabilities: deterministic sequencing, intelligent workload partitioning, data versioning, latest-state processing, and self-healing recovery.  Attendees will explore how partitioning transactions by business identifiers enables parallel execution while ensuring controlled processing of updates affecting shared records. Version-aware processing helps identify stale updates and prevents outdated information from overwriting newer data. Latest-state synchronization further improves resilience by retrieving authoritative business data instead of relying on potentially delayed intermediate events.  The session also examines recovery mechanisms that preserve failed transaction metadata, evaluate retry conditions, retrieve current record states, and isolate persistent failures for investigation.  Attendees will leave with practical design patterns for handling concurrency failures, strengthening data consistency, improving recovery reliability, and building distributed systems that remain predictable under challenging operating conditions.
