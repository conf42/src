# AIOps: New Approaches to Reduce Alert Noise

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2024](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2024) by [Birol Yildiz](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Birol_Yildiz) (Co-Founder & CEO @ Ilert). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2024_Birol_Yildiz_aiops_alert_noise

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YKuhDxdHuME

## Abstract

We will dive into techniques to group alerts based on content similarity with the help of AI and cover approaches to adjusting alert thresholds to find a balance between capturing all potential duplicates and minimizing false positives.
