# Leveraging Digital Innovation for Environmental Compliance: A Path to Sustainability & Excellence

A talk at [Conf42 Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) 2025](https://www.conf42.com/sre2025) by [Sneha Kalagarla](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sneha_Kalagarla) (Cleveland State University). ## Abstract

Discover how digital platforms are transforming environmental protection! From AI-driven tools to real-time monitoring, this talk unveils strategies that enhance efficiency, cut costs, and drive sustainability. Join us to explore the future of smarter, greener regulatory systems!
