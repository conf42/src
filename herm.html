Your Agent Passed the Prompt Test. Will It Survive Production?

A great prompt doesn't guarantee a reliable AI agent. What happens when your agent encounters unexpected inputs, real users, changing environments, or failures in production? In this session, we'll explore why prompt testing alone isn't enough and how developers can test, monitor, and improve AI agents for real-world conditions.
