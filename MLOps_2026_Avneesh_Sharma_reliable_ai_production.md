# When Ground Truth Arrives Late: Building Reliable AI Systems in Production

A talk at [Conf42 MLOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/mlops2026) by [Avneesh Sharma](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Avneesh_Sharma) (Machine Learning Engineer @ Amazon). Page: https://www.conf42.com/MLOps_2026_Avneesh_Sharma_reliable_ai_production

## Abstract

Modern AI systems are expected to make decisions using information scattered across databases, APIs, and continuously changing data pipelines. But what happens when the ground truth changes after an AI system has already made a decision? This talk explores the engineering challenges of building reliable AI systems when data arrives late, evidence conflicts, and information becomes stale. Drawing from practical experience building large-scale ML and AI infrastructure, we'll explore evidence versioning, temporal consistency, claim-level validation, and evaluation strategies that go beyond traditional model accuracy. Attendees will learn practical architectural patterns for building AI systems that remain reliable even when their underlying data doesn't.
