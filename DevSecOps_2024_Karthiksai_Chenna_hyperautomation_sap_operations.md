# Revolutionizing SAP Production Planning with Hyperautomation: Secure, Efficient, and Insight-Driven Operations

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2024](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2024) by [Karthiksai Chenna](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Karthiksai_Chenna) (Rich Products). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2024_Karthiksai_Chenna_hyperautomation_sap_operations

## Abstract

Discover how hyperautomation is transforming SAP Production Planning with RPA, AI, ML, and Process Mining to boost efficiency, cut costs, and ensure secure, real-time operations. Learn from real-world success stories and unlock the secrets to driving 25-40% productivity gains.
