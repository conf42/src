# The hidden infra behind "For You" pages: Real-time AI personalization

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2026](https://www.conf42.com/platform2026) by [Attila Toth](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Attila_Toth) (Senior Developer Educator @ ScyllaDB). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2026_Attila_Toth_personalization_infra_pages

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KCmFRH9CVwo

## Abstract

AI personalization for apps with a large and global user base is a difficult engineering challenge. Global companies like Tripadvisor, Medium, and ShareChat depend on highly available, low-latency AI infrastructure to deliver hotel recommendations, "For You" feed items and relevant short-form video content. In this talk, we cover the underlying systems that power these user facing recommendation use cases. - Running real-time model inference with millisecond latency - Setting up a centralized feature store that feeds both training and serving - Leveraging OSS feature store frameworks and tools - Best-practice data modeling approaches for online inference to minimize latency - Cost-effective utilization of cold/object storage, hot caches, and low-latency databases for model training and user-facing inference - Meeting strict SLAs (p90, p95, p99, etc) By the end, attendees will learn how to design AI infrastructure that serves fresh features in milliseconds. They will also see real data models, latency numbers, and cost trade-offs from three production systems.
