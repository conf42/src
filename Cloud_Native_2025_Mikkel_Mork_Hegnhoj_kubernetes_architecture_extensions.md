# Introduction to SpinKube

A talk at [Conf42 Cloud Native 2025](https://www.conf42.com/cloud2025) by [Mikkel Mork Hegnhoj](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Mikkel_Mork_Hegnhoj) (Head of Product and DevRel @ Fermyon). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Cloud_Native_2025_Mikkel_Mork_Hegnhoj_kubernetes_architecture_extensions

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wJhB12q1yOY

## Abstract

SpinKube streamlines developing, deploying and operating Spin WebAssembly applications in Kubernetes, resulting in delivering smaller, and more portable applications. The session will also introduce you to Spin and SpinKube, and compare and contrast the benefits and trade-off compared to containers.
