# Compliance as a Pipeline Stage: Automating AI Governance in MLOps

A talk at [Conf42 MLOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/mlops2026) by [Arpita Sheth](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Arpita_Sheth) (Software Engineer @ Software Engineer). Page: https://www.conf42.com/MLOps_2026_Arpita_Sheth_compliance_governance_pipeline

## Abstract

Most ML teams treat compliance as a review that happens before launch. Under the EU AI Act, U.S. AI governance frameworks, and privacy laws, that approach breaks down, because models are retrained, redeployed, and updated continuously.  This talk shows how to move governance into the MLOps pipeline itself. Drawing on experience building automated GDPR compliance systems, ROPA reporting pipelines, and data governance platforms at Meta, Arpita covers:  Risk classification at model registration Lineage capture across datasets, features, and model versions Policy checks as CI/CD gates before training and deployment Monitoring signals that feed compliance evidence Documentation generated automatically from pipeline metadata  The talk also covers practical trade-offs, including where automated gates slow teams down and how to keep engineering and compliance aligned without manual handoffs.
