# AI-Driven Cybersecurity: Intelligent Threat Detection and Privacy in the Post-Quantum Era

A talk at [Conf42 MLOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/mlops2025) by [Arfi Siddik Mollashaik](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Arfi_Siddik_Mollashaik) (Solution Architect - Professional Services - Data Security, Data Privacy and Data Governance @ securiti.ai). Page: https://www.conf42.com/MLOps_2025_Arfi_Siddik_Mollashaik_ai_driven_cybersecurity

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2wMBKhYm3hI

## Abstract

Discover how AI and ML are revolutionizing cybersecurity with real-time threat detection, privacy-preserving models, and quantum-resistant defenses. This talk unveils the future of secure, intelligent systems built to protect against today’s threats and tomorrow’s quantum risks.
