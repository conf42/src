# Platform Migrations Without the Downtime: An Engineering Playbook

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2026](https://www.conf42.com/platform2026) by [Saikrishna Ala](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Saikrishna_Ala) (Senior Applications Developer @ Bath and Body Works). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2026_Saikrishna_Ala_migrations_downtime_playbook

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H7dZ8BoJdpM

## Abstract

Platform engineering teams are increasingly the ones owning the hardest kind of project: replacing a legacy system the business depends on every hour of every day. This talk presents a practitioner-built architecture playbook for exactly that problem, drawn from real migrations of enterprise order management, healthcare claims, and CRM platforms. It covers three core patterns platform teams can build as reusable internal capability — dual-write parallel-run architecture, bidirectional synchronization for legacy/new system consistency, and change data capture for scalable incremental data movement — along with a five-dimension maturity framework (latency, reliability, bidirectionality, scalability, migration safety) for assessing whether an internal platform is actually ready to support a major cutover. Forrester-cited data shows event-driven approaches can cut data latency from hours to milliseconds; attendees will leave with criteria for building this as platform capability rather than reinventing it project by project.
