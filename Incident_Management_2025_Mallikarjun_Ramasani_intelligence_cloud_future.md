# Intelligent Incident Management: AI, ML, and the Future of Resilient Cloud Operations

A talk at [Conf42 Incident Management 2025](https://www.conf42.com/im2025) by [Mallikarjun Ramasani](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Mallikarjun_Ramasani) (Software Engineer @ Barclays). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Incident_Management_2025_Mallikarjun_Ramasani_intelligence_cloud_future

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=10wvHoa_32s

## Abstract

AI is revolutionizing incident response. Learn how ML-driven detection, root cause analysis, and auto-remediation are cutting downtime and boosting resilience in cloud ops. A must-see for SREs and platform teams building fault-tolerant systems.
