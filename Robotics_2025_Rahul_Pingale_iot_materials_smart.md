# Smart Non-Woven Surfaces: IoT-Enabled Materials Engineering for Sustainable, Connected Environments

A talk at [Conf42 Robotics 2025](https://www.conf42.com/robotics2025) by [Rahul Pingale](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Rahul_Pingale) (Technical Director @ Wallquest). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Robotics_2025_Rahul_Pingale_iot_materials_smart

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gGil46sxZJg

## Abstract

Discover how a sustainable, PVC-free non-woven surface platform integrates IoT, sensors, and automation. Validated across ASTM, FINAT, and CDPH standards, it enables smart robotics environments with barrier coatings, adhesive tech, and embedded connectivity.
