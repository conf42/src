# Observability as a Platform Contract: Zero-Code Instrumentation and CI/CD-Enforced SLOs

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Sumit Kaul](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sumit_Kaul) (Staff Software Engineer @ Payjoy). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Sumit_Kaul_slos_instrumentation_observability

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pQBoWM78Q5w

## Abstract

Stop treating observability as optional tooling. Learn how to build platform contracts that enforce zero-code instrumentation and CI/CD-gated SLOs—so every service ships with traces, metrics, and reliability guarantees baked in. Practical patterns from finance, logistics, and retail environments.
