A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Swapna Chimanchodkar](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Swapna_Chimanchodkar) (Senior Consultant, Manager, Cognizant Business Consulting @ Osmania University). ## Abstract

Deploy AI that saves lives. Learn how DevOps practices power real-time sepsis detection in production healthcare environments. Kubernetes, observability, and CI/CD patterns for mission-critical ML systems where downtime means mortality. Healthcare DevOps challenges mirror fintech complexity.
