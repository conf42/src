# Empowering Healthcare with AI-Driven IoT: Enhancing Accessibility, Personalization, and Data Integrity

A talk at [Conf42 Internet of Things (IoT) 2024](https://www.conf42.com/iot2024) by [Abhishek Vajpayee](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Abhishek_Vajpayee) (Senior Analytics Engineer/Product Owner @ Metropolis Technologies) and [Rathish Mohan](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Rathish_Mohan) (Senior Applied ML Scientist @ Lore | Contagious Health). ## Abstract

Discover how IoT-powered AI chatbots are reshaping healthcare! By blending real-time data from wearables and EHRs with generative AI, these bots deliver 24/7, personalized care that breaks geographic barriers, eases emergency loads, and enhances patient outcomes—all while safeguarding data privacy.
