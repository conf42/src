## Abstract

Kubernetes Secrets by default aren't secure. In fact, some Kubernetes documentation itself even recommends taking a look at a third-party utility. In short it's because secrets are stored in plain-text by default.
In this session, you'll learn how to fix that issue by combining Kubernetes and Vault
