# GenOps: A Governance-Driven Model for Safe, High-Velocity AI in CI/CD Pipelines

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Neeraj Kumar Singh Beshane](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Neeraj_Kumar_Singh_Beshane) (Staff Software Engineer @ Meta). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Neeraj_Kumar_Singh_Beshane_governance_genai_velocity

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YcGxfYXwUvI

## Abstract

Want AI-powered automation without AI-powered outages? GenOps introduces a governance-first model that lets generative agents act inside CI/CD pipelines safely, predictably, and fast. Discover how teams cut cycle time while staying fully compliant.
