# Artificial Intelligence: Empowering Students, Not Replacing Universities

A talk at [Conf42 Machine Learning 2025](https://www.conf42.com/ml2025) by [Vahid Mohammadi](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Vahid_Mohammadi) (Founder @ Universitio). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Machine_Learning_2025_Vahid_Mohammadi_universities_students_intelligence

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EmuqhJZYh0Y

## Abstract

AI empowers students with smart tools while reinforcing—not replacing—universities. Drawing from real startup experience, Vahid Mohammadi explores how responsible AI use enhances learning, integrity, and institutional impact.
