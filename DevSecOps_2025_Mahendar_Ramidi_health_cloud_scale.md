# Securing Healthcare at Scale: DevSecOps for Critical Patient Platforms

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2025) by [Mahendar Ramidi](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Mahendar_Ramidi) (Software Developer @ Maryland Health Benefits Exchange). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2025_Mahendar_Ramidi_health_cloud_scale

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zgdf3Zhfmf8

## Abstract

I built a HIPAA-compliant mobile platform serving 450+ cancer patients across 12 centers. Learn my DevSecOps approach: zero-trust architecture, compliance automation, and secure CI/CD that achieved 92% completion rates.
