{# ── conf42.com/record (hidden speaker tool, Marek 2026-10-22): the talk recorder that replaces OBS. The event's New design overlay (assets/js/overlays.js, the teasers' Overlays tab) frames the recording: the camera in the circle, the slides or a shared screen in the window; no camera or nothing in the window = the one big window, a second camera = co-talk. The engine is assets/js/record-worker.js (1080p60 H.264 + AAC MP4, written to the speaker's disk as it records, with a copy in the browser's storage). Nothing is uploaded. Round 2 (Marek 2026-10-22): the conf42.com look (Plus Jakarta Sans, the dark navy hero, vivid colours), one screen with no scrolling, and a guided setup in 3 steps on opening - 1. microphone (choose + test), 2. camera (on screen or not, which one), 3. what fills the window (PDF slides, a screen, both, or just you). Finishing it turns the same controls into the settings column, each step one click away. Chrome and Edge only: anything else gets a "please change your browser" screen - a soft one ("Try anyway") for other Chromium browsers that have every feature (Brave, Opera, Arc...), a hard one for Safari, Firefox and phones.
