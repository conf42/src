# Access is the New Breach: Why Observability Must Start with Identity

A talk at [Conf42 Observability 2025](https://www.conf42.com/obs2025) by [Cynthia Akiotu](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Cynthia_Akiotu) (Cyber Risk Analyst @ Tesco Bank). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Cynthia_Akiotu_identity_access_breach

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_CbvYGGESjM

## Abstract

Access is the new breach. Yet most observability tools ignore who’s doing what. This talk shows how to integrate identity into observability—so you can detect privilege misuse, insider threats, and audit violations before they become headlines.
