## Abstract

Unlock the secrets to modernizing financial systems at scale! Learn how Microsoft cut costs by 25%, improved data accuracy by 40%, and sped up time-to-market by 50% with Cloud, AI, and metadata. Discover strategies to boost efficiency, agility, and smarter financial decisions!
