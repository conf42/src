# Kube-Native Automation for Equitable and Resilient Digital Public Services

A talk at [Conf42 Kube Native 2025](https://www.conf42.com/kubenative2025) by [Mahesh Jalukuri](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Mahesh_Jalukuri) (Senior DevOps Engineer @ Envoy Global). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Kube_Native_2025_Mahesh_Jalukuri_automation_publicservices_resilience

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HuycpQFb1wo

## Abstract

Discover how Kubernetes-native automation is powering equitable, high-impact digital public services from slashing deployment times to boosting resilience in healthcare, legal, and utility systems. Learn from real-world wins and walk away with a framework for inclusive, scalable transformation.
