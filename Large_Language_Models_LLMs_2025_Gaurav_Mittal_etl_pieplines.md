# Leveraging AI to Optimize ETL Pipelines

A talk at [Conf42 Large Language Models (LLMs) 2025](https://www.conf42.com/llms2025) by [Gaurav Mittal](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Gaurav_Mittal) (Software Development Engineer @ Nordstrom). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Large_Language_Models_LLMs_2025_Gaurav_Mittal_etl_pieplines

## Abstract

Discover how AI is revolutionizing Extract, Transform, Load (ETL) processes in modern data management for increased efficiency, accuracy, and scalability. Explore concepts and tools.
