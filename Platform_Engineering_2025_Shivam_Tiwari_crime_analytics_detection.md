# Building Real-Time Analytics Platforms for Financial Crime Detection

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/platform2025) by [Shivam Tiwari](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Shivam_Tiwari) (The University of Texas at Dallas). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2025_Shivam_Tiwari_crime_analytics_detection

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y-QRB0dKkIg

## Abstract

Build the infrastructure that stops financial crime! Learn platform engineering patterns for real-time analytics systems processing millions of transactions with sub-second detection. High-stakes engineering at scale!
