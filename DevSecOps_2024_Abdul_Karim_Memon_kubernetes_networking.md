# Kubernetes Networking 101

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2024](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2024) by [Abdul Karim Memon](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Abdul_Karim_Memon) (Co-Founder @ ReLambda). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2024_Abdul_Karim_Memon_kubernetes_networking

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fNVAyIw5ohw

## Abstract

A beginner-friendly talk that explains how networking in Kubernetes works under the hood. It provides examples of the process of IP address management and helps you understand the journey of a packet as it travels from one pod to another within a Kubernetes (K8s) cluster.
