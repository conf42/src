# Observability in Kubernetes: Unlocking the Power of eBPF

A talk at [Conf42 Kube Native 2025](https://www.conf42.com/kubenative2025) by [Alejandro Mercado](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Alejandro_Mercado) (DevOps Lead @ Michael Page). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Kube_Native_2025_Alejandro_Mercado_observability_kubernetes_ebpf

## Abstract

Discover how eBPF-powered observability can facilitate proactive incident detection, root cause analysis, and rapid remediation, enabling you to stay ahead of potential issues in your Kubernetes ecosystem.
