# Enhancing IoT-Enabled Self-Service Kiosks with iOS Devices: A Data-Driven Approach to Contactless Interactions

A talk at [Conf42 Internet of Things (IoT) 2024](https://www.conf42.com/iot2024) by [Sai Krishna Paladugu](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sai_Krishna_Paladugu) (iOS Mobile Specialist @ Fedex). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Internet_of_Things_IoT_2024_Sai_Krishna_Paladugu_kiosk_ios

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fJuWXSpzWyc

## Abstract

Discover how iOS-powered kiosks are transforming industries through contactless IoT integration! From Apple Pay to QR scanning, learn how businesses are boosting efficiency, cutting transaction times by 30%, and enhancing customer experiences with personalized, secure, and data-driven solutions.
