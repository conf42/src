# Agentic AI, CI/CD Automation, and the Future of DevOps

Conf42 Blog, 2026-01-23. https://www.conf42.com/2026-01-22-harness

In short: Explore how AI is redefining DevOps practices across CI/CD, infrastructure, and operations

The DevOps landscape is entering a new phase where AI-driven automation, agentic systems, and intelligent CI/CD pipelines are becoming core to software delivery. As organizations scale cloud-native architectures and accelerate release velocity, traditional DevOps approaches struggle to keep pace. Conf42 DevOps 2026 brings together industry experts to explore how AI is redefining DevOps practices across CI/CD, infrastructure, and operations.

This virtual conference is designed for DevOps engineers, platform teams, SREs, and engineering leaders who want practical insights into enterprise-ready DevOps powered by AI. The sessions focus on real-world adoption, architectural patterns, and governance strategies that help teams move from experimentation to production.

## Chinmay Gaikwad Keynote on Agentic AI in CI/CD

The featured keynote at Conf42 DevOps 2026 is delivered by [Chinmay Gaikwad](https://www.linkedin.com/in/chinmay-gaikwad/), Director of Product Marketing at Harness. His session, *(Building Enterprise-ready CI/CD Pipelines using Agentic AI](https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Chinmay_Gaikwad_cicd_agentic_ai)*, examines how agentic AI transforms modern CI/CD pipelines.

Chinmay explains how AI agents can autonomously reason across pipeline context, detect and remediate failures, enforce security and compliance, and continuously optimize delivery performance. The keynote also addresses enterprise concerns such as scalability, reliability, and governance, making it highly relevant for organizations modernizing their DevOps platforms.

For teams evaluating [AI-powered CI/CD automation](https://www.harness.io/products/continuous-delivery/ai-assisted-deployment-verification), this keynote provides a clear and actionable roadmap.

## DevOps Sessions Featuring AI Agents and Automation

Conf42 DevOps 2026 features multiple sessions that showcase how AI agents are being applied across the DevOps lifecycle.

**Premanand Tiwari** presents a session on autonomous time-series forecasting, demonstrating how AI-driven systems can improve observability and operational decision-making without manual intervention.

**Pallab Haldar**, Technical Architect, shares insights on AI-powered workflow orchestration, focusing on how intelligent automation can dynamically manage complex DevOps pipelines and toolchains.

**Gobu Natarajan**, Manager of Technical Program Management, explores multimodal conversational agents and their role in correlating logs, metrics, and human input to accelerate incident response.

**Praneeth Patil**, Senior Staff Software Engineer, discusses autonomous infrastructure management in cloud environments and how AI agents can improve scalability, resilience, and cost efficiency.
