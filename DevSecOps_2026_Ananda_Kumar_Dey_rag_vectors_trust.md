# Secure Enterprise RAG: Building Trusted Vectorized Knowledge Systems for DevSecOps

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2026) by [Ananda Kumar Dey](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Ananda_Kumar_Dey) (Sr. Director - Solutions @ Quadrant Technologies LLC). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2026_Ananda_Kumar_Dey_rag_vectors_trust

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WFpTX__ugsM

## Abstract

Modern DevSecOps environments generate large volumes of documentation, operational knowledge, security artifacts, and deployment data that must be accessible without compromising governance or trust. Traditional keyword-based search often fails to retrieve relevant information across these distributed knowledge sources, limiting operational efficiency and slowing incident response. This session explores how Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and vectorized knowledge architectures enable secure, production-ready enterprise knowledge platforms for modern engineering organizations.  The presentation examines practical architectural patterns for building enterprise RAG systems, including document ingestion, embedding generation, vector indexing, hybrid retrieval, and context-aware response generation. It discusses how security controls, identity-aware access, encryption, governance policies, and auditability can be integrated throughout the retrieval pipeline to protect sensitive enterprise knowledge while improving developer productivity. The session also covers techniques for reducing hallucinations through grounded retrieval, trusted data sources, retrieval evaluation, and human oversight.  Drawing from enterprise architecture practices and production implementation patterns, the presentation highlights lessons learned in designing scalable, cloud-native AI knowledge systems that support engineering, operations, and security teams. Emerging approaches such as agentic workflows and graph-enhanced retrieval are also discussed to demonstrate how organizations can improve reasoning across complex information ecosystems. Attendees will leave with practical guidance for designing secure, trustworthy, and operationally resilient AI knowledge platforms that align with DevSecOps principles.
