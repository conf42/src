# Building Resilient AI Pricing Platforms: Engineering Real-Time Decision Systems at Scale

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/platform2025) by [Amaresh Sahoo](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Amaresh_Sahoo) (Vice President, Pricing, Sourcing & Analytics @ Xometry). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2025_Amaresh_Sahoo_pricing_decision_time

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8mejpGTmxgQ

## Abstract

Learn how to build millisecond-response AI pricing platforms that handle millions of decisions daily. We'll cover edge computing, ML pipelines, circuit breakers, and MLOps patterns from real retail systems serving global customers at scale.
