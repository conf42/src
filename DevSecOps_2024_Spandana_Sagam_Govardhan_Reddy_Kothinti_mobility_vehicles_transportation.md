# Securing the Future of Mobility: Autonomous Vehicles, Safety, and the Path to Smarter Transportation

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2024](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2024) by [Spandana Sagam](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Spandana_Sagam) (Senior HIL Development Engineer Autonomous & Automated Driving @ General Motors) and [Govardhan Reddy Kothinti](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Govardhan_Reddy_Kothinti) (Functional Safety Manager @ APTIV LLC). ## Abstract

Discover how autonomous vehicles are set to revolutionize mobility, cut traffic accidents by 94%, and transform cities with safer roads and greener spaces. Explore the tech behind AVs, their economic impact, and the security challenges ahead. Join us to unlock the future of smarter mobility.
