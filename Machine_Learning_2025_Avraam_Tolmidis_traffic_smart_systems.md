# AI on the Fast Lane: Smart Traffic Systems to Unclog our Cities

A talk at [Conf42 Machine Learning 2025](https://www.conf42.com/ml2025) by [Avraam Tolmidis](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Avraam_Tolmidis) (Software Engineering Manager @ Meta). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Machine_Learning_2025_Avraam_Tolmidis_traffic_smart_systems

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ylpODQKnGL0

## Abstract

Urban congestion costs time, money, and the planet. Discover how AI, multi-agent systems, and predictive optimization can turn traffic chaos into flow, using real-world applications that bridge cutting-edge research and scalable, city-wide solutions.
