## Abstract

Discover how Layer 2 & Layer 3 networking, VLANs, OSPF, BGP, MPLS, and QoS optimize network performance and security. Join Ravi Theja Kambhampati to explore real-world applications of routing, switching, ACLs, and IPsec in building scalable, resilient networks for enterprise, IoT, and 5G.
