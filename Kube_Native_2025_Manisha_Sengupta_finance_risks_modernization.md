# Securing AI-Driven Finance: Navigating Risks in Cloud-Native Modernization

A talk at [Conf42 Kube Native 2025](https://www.conf42.com/kubenative2025) by [Manisha Sengupta](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Manisha_Sengupta) (Assistant Vice President Systems, Investor Services Technology @ Brown Brothers Harriman). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Kube_Native_2025_Manisha_Sengupta_finance_risks_modernization

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QH7uUDXcmyk

## Abstract

Discover how AI is transforming finance and why cloud-native adoption brings hidden risks. Learn real-world attack vectors, cutting-edge defenses like XAI and anomaly detection, and a practical roadmap to secure AI-driven systems without slowing innovation.
