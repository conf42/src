# DO-178C in Multicore Avionics: Certification Paths for Electric Aircraft

A talk at [Conf42 Robotics 2025](https://www.conf42.com/robotics2025) by [Charles Antony Raj](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Charles_Antony_Raj) (Senior Manager, Systems & Software Engineering @ Collins Aerospace). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Robotics_2025_Charles_Antony_Raj_avionics_certify_flight

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N-dEvazVukg

## Abstract

Discover how electric aircraft projects are overcoming multicore certification hurdles under DO-178C. Learn proven strategies to cut delays, ensure safety, and accelerate compliance vital for the future of robotic and electric aviation.
