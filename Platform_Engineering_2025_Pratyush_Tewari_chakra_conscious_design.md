# Chakra UX: Conscious Design for Ethical, Sustainable, and Scalable Platforms

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/platform2025) by [Pratyush Tewari](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Pratyush_Tewari) (Head of UX Design @ Blue Book Services). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2025_Pratyush_Tewari_chakra_conscious_design

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EvRH8huj-Bg

## Abstract

Discover how ancient wisdom meets modern platform engineering. This talk unveils the Chakra UX framework infusing ethics, sustainability, and human well-being into design. Learn to build systems that are not just scalable, but deeply conscious and impactful.
