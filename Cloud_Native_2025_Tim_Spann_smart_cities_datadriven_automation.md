# Smart Cities Unleashed

A talk at [Conf42 Cloud Native 2025](https://www.conf42.com/cloud2025) by [Tim Spann](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Tim_Spann) (Senior Solutions Engineer @ Snowflake). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Cloud_Native_2025_Tim_Spann_smart_cities_datadriven_automation

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yqHKnP8-D2g

## Abstract

Discover how AI, NiFi, Snowflake, Iceberg are revolutionizing city data analytics with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). In this session, we’ll explore how these cloud-native tools transform vast urban datasets into actionable insights, enabling smarter decision-making in real time. From optimizing traffic flows to enhancing public services, learn how to harness cutting-edge AI and vector search technology to tackle the unique challenges of modern city management. Unlock the future of smart cities and elevate urban innovation to new heights!
