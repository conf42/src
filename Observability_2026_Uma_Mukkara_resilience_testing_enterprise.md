# Green Dashboards, Broken Systems: Why Observability Alone Can't Prove Resilience

A talk at [Conf42 Observability 2026](https://www.conf42.com/obs2026) by [Uma Mukkara](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Uma_Mukkara) (Head of Resilience Testing @ Harness). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2026_Uma_Mukkara_resilience_testing_enterprise

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OORwIuqgKV8

## Abstract

Observability can tell you what your system is doing, but it cannot prove how it will behave when something breaks. Dashboards may stay green while hidden dependencies, degraded services, or unexpected failure modes quietly put users at risk. This talk explores the gap between visibility and resilience, and why teams need to actively test failure scenarios rather than rely on monitoring alone.
