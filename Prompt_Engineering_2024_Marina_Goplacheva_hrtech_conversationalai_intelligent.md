A talk at [Conf42 Prompt Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/prompt2024) by [Marina Goplacheva](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Marina_Goplacheva) (Product Owner, Conversational AI, HR Transformation Department @ Sberbank). ## Abstract

Unlock the future of HR with AI! In How to Build Intelligent Conversational AI Experiences, I'll show you how to harness chatbots and virtual assistants to transform recruitment, onboarding, and employee engagement—making your HR processes smarter, faster, and more human-centric.
