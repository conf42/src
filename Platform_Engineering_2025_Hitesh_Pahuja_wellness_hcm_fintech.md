# Architecting Scalable Platforms for Financial Wellness Through HCM–FinTech Integration

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/platform2025) by [Hitesh Pahuja](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Hitesh_Pahuja) (Senior Software Engineering Manager @ Intuit). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2025_Hitesh_Pahuja_wellness_hcm_fintech

## Abstract

Discover how platform engineering can transform workforce wellbeing! Learn to integrate HCM and FinTech systems using scalable, privacy-first architectures that boost employee engagement, reduce stress, and deliver real ROI all through smart, secure design.
