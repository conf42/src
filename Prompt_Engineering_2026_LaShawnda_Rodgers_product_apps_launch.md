## Abstract

Prompting is not the beginning of bringing your idea to life it starts long before your first prompt. This session will have attendees leaving with not just understanding the power of their prompt in development but also:  1.  How to create a repeatable process when building a product 2. Participants will understand how to go from novice prompt to expert 3. How to make sure your prompt keeps your product in scope and recognize when it hallucinates.  4. The attendees will leave with a sense of understanding how to use tools like Resend, OpenAi Realtime, APiIs, and other tools for integration and how they fit in the process.
