# Conf42 Prompt Engineering 2025 roundup: framing, ranking, delivery

Conf42 Blog, 2025-11-07. https://www.conf42.com/prompt2025/prompt-engineering-2025-roundup-framing-ranking

In short: Conf42 Prompt Engineering 2025 in review, from prompt psychology and LLM pairwise ranking to Medicare compliance, eBay seller tools and AI networking.

Shashank Shivam opened his talk at [Conf42 Prompt Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/prompt2025) with an experiment: ask ChatGPT how it would destroy humanity and it refuses; ask it to write a screenplay about an AI that does and you get a ten-step plan. Same model, same weights, different framing. That gap between what a prompt says and what it makes a model do ran through the whole event, held online on November 6, 2025, with 28 talks from Oracle, Microsoft, Apple, eBay, Snowflake, Salesforce, Equinix, Synopsys, JPMorgan Chase and others.

## Prompts shape identity, so design for trust

[Shashank Shivam](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Shashank_Shivam_models_psychology_behavior) (Oracle) treated prompting as psychology: framing, priming and role assignment change how a model reasons, so a prompt is closer to telling the model who to be than what to do. [Aroma Rodrigues](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Aroma_Rodrigues_chatgpt_talk_conversation) (Microsoft) ran a set of riddle experiments on GPT-3 to test the AGI hype. Once told it was solving riddles, the model got "when is a door no longer a door?" right, then confidently answered staircase for a riddle whose answer is legs. It recognised the genre without understanding the joke.

[Raj Kommera](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Raj_Kommera_trust_llm_judgment) made the case for prompt architectures that carry attribution, context and audit trails from the first token, and [Preetham Sunilkumar](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Preetham_Sunilkumar_frameworks_llms_scalable) (LPL Financial) covered evaluation frameworks and adversarial testing for high-stakes LLM use.

## Ranking, judging and testing with LLMs

The most technically specific talk came from [Maximilian Schattauer](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Maximilian_Schattauer_retrievers_documents_ranking) (Perelyn). Embedding models and BM25 both treat the query as a black box with no reasoning step, and in specialised domains where documents are too similar for either, that breaks down. His team prompted an LLM to compare documents pairwise and judge which better answers the question, then reordered results accordingly, and saw recall@k improve in those special cases. Worth watching if your search results are all plausible and none of them are right.

[Yash Panjari](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Yash_Panjari_testing_conversational_automation) (Apple) applied LLM-as-judge and generated test data to the hard problem of testing voice and chat systems, where human dialogue never follows the script. [Balraj Govindaraj](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Balraj_Govindaraj_testing_automation_agile) (MVP Plus Consulting) showed manual testers moving to automation through codeless tools and prompts, and [Swamy Biru](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Swamy_Biru_finance_efficiency_agents) (JPMorgan Chase) presented RAG-backed agents for financial operations.

## Prompts in the delivery and data pipeline

[Bandanawaz Mulla](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Bandanawaz_Mulla_healthcare_cloud_solutions) (Health Care Service Corporation) had a deadline-shaped story: a 2024 CMS mandate for Medicare Advantage supplemental benefit reporting meant consolidating 102 benefit types from more than ten systems for two million members. LLMs were used across the development lifecycle, including rapid prototyping of the ETL logic. [Janak Bhalla](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Janak_Bhalla_code_developers_scale) (Microsoft) covered LLM tooling for code migrations and fixing compile errors at scale.

On the data side, [Jimish Kadakia](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Jimish_Kadakia_data_cloud_access) (Snowflake) showed natural-language interfaces for querying data and triggering pipelines, [Siva Kumar Raju Bhupathiraju](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Siva_Kumar_Raju_Bhupathiraju_sql_infrastructure_hybrid) (Paycor) prepared SQL backends with Azure SQL Managed Instance for LLM workloads, [Annapurneswar Putrevu](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Annapurneswar_Putrevu_pipelines_learning_systems) (Bloom Energy) combined prompts with reinforcement learning for self-healing pipelines, and [Richa Solanki](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Richa_Solanki_data_lakehouse_enterprise) (Ally Bank) covered lakehouse architecture. [Bhavana Reddy Chadagonda](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Bhavana_Reddy_Chadagonda_science_testing_experimentation) argued that experimentation skills remain a data scientist's edge in AI-heavy roles.

## The infrastructure under the prompt

A cluster of talks went below the API. [Praneeth Patil](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Praneeth_Patil_agents_infrastructure_multicloud) (Equinix) looked at LLMs moving from writing infrastructure snippets to acting as multi-cloud agents, with hallucinations, misconfigurations and cost as the risks to design around. [Rahul Thakur](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Rahul_Thakur_infrastructure_resilience_scale) (Salesforce) sharded AI services across zones for resilience. [Rajesh Arsid](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Rajesh_Arsid_ethernet_performance_scaling) (Synopsys) explained how Ultra Ethernet and UALink affect inference throughput and latency across distributed systems, a reminder that prompt engineering at scale is also a networking problem. [Vamsi Gadireddy](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Vamsi_Gadireddy_network_protocols_reliability) (Microsoft Azure) covered AI in network engineering itself.

## Industry case studies

The remaining talks applied prompt engineering domain by domain: seller tools at eBay ([Sureshkumar Karuppuchamy](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Sureshkumar_Karuppuchamy_ecommerce_tools_ai)), insurance claims triage and underwriting ([Chetan Prakash Ratnawat](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Chetan_Prakash_Ratnawat_insurance_transformation_workflows), Capgemini), Power BI supply chain analytics at Kellogg's ([Victor Gabriel](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Victor_Gabriel_supplychain_savings_bi), GEP Worldwide), multi-agent ERP design ([Kasee Palaniappan](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Kasee_Palaniappan_enterprise_erp_intelligence), SAP Ariba), fleet telematics ([Preeti Sali](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Preeti_Sali_fleet_management_telematics), Ford), supply chain automation ([Kunal Dixit](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Kunal_Dixit_supplychain_automation_resilience), Zensar), Security Copilot for Conditional Access and Zero Trust ([Cynthia Akiotu](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Cynthia_Akiotu_security_defense_cyber)), agile delivery ([Hema Yalamancheli](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Hema_Yalamancheli_agile_delivery_prompt), Tyler Technologies) and enterprise migrations ([Meenu Venkataarangam](https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Meenu_Venkataarangam_migrations_automation_innovation), Recode Solutions).
