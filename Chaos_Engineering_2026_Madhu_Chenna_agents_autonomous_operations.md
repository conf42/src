# When the Fixer Breaks: Failure-Mode Thinking for Autonomous IT Operations Agents

A talk at [Conf42 Chaos Engineering 2026](https://www.conf42.com/chaos2026) by [Madhu Chenna](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Madhu_Chenna) (Senior Manager @ Electronic Arts). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Chaos_Engineering_2026_Madhu_Chenna_agents_autonomous_operations

## Abstract

Agentic AI promises to shift IT operations from reactive ticket handling to predicting and fixing problems before users notice. But an agent that can remediate an endpoint can also remediate the wrong one. Resilience has to cover the automation itself, not only the systems it manages.  This talk uses the Autonomous Digital Operations Framework (ADOF), a conceptual six-layer architecture, as a case for failure-mode thinking. ADOF combines endpoint telemetry, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and six specialized agents, including Incident Prediction, Asset Health, and Procurement Intelligence. The talk covers:  Failure scenarios worth testing: stale or missing telemetry, outdated knowledge returned by retrieval, and incorrect predictions How composite scores (Enterprise Health, Business Impact, and Automation Confidence) serve as a safety boundary, with agents acting above 85% confidence and escalating below 60% Why confidence should drop, not hold steady, when input data degrades How a five-level maturity model limits the impact radius of agent errors while autonomy grows  ADOF is proposed for future validation. Viewers will leave with a practical checklist of failure modes to test before giving AI agents operational authority.
