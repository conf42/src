# Securing Robot Networks for the Quantum Era

A talk at [Conf42 Remix 2026](https://www.conf42.com/remix2026) by [Nehal Singh](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Nehal_Singh) (Sr. Manager, IT Infrastructure & Productivity Apps @ California State University, Long Beach). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Remix_2026_Nehal_Singh_robotics_quantum_security

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yPdOXma-pcM

## Abstract

Quantum computers could soon compromise robotic systems. Learn how to secure your robot networks with post-quantum cryptography, using NIST standards and real-world case studies without breaking real-time performance.
