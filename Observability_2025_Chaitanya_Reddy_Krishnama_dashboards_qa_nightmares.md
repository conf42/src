# Data QA Nightmares: A Journey from Broken Dashboards to Observability Maturity

A talk at [Conf42 Observability 2025](https://www.conf42.com/obs2025) by [Chaitanya Reddy Krishnama](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Chaitanya_Reddy_Krishnama) (Moneygram Inc). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Chaitanya_Reddy_Krishnama_dashboards_qa_nightmares

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HN7PtUCR23c

## Abstract

Our dashboards looked great until they started lying. In this talk, I’ll share how hidden data issues, silent pipeline failures, and schema drift broke our analytics and how we fixed it by combining observability with automated QA.
