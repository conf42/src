# Health Checks for Systems. What About Your Team?

A talk at [Conf42 Observability 2026](https://www.conf42.com/obs2026) by [Sylvain Kalache](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sylvain_Kalache) (Head of AI Labs @ Rootly). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2026_Sylvain_Kalache_burnout_oncall_sre

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fu97UJfOoIA

## Abstract

We monitor everything: liveness probes, SLOs, a dashboard for every service. Yet the engineers holding the pager get a single health check: "How are you doing?" And the answer is always "Fine." This talk applies system-level discipline to team health. Taking inspiration from research on the topic, we'll see how the signals your stack already emits can be turned into an early-warning system for overwork. Then we'll go inside large organizations that run this approach in production today: how they caught unsustainable rotations their dashboards missed, what changed for their on-call teams, and the lessons they learned rolling it out. You'll leave with a practical framework to spot overwork before it turns into resignations.
