    Abstract

    Modern engineering teams are increasingly adopting automation and intelligent systems to improve operational reliability, security, and governance. However, managing compliance risks across global workforce operations remains challenging when organizations rely on manual reviews and fragmented monitoring processes. This session shares a real-world case study of building and deploying a Machine Learning-powered Working Hours Alert System (WHAS) to automate compliance monitoring and detect potential time fraud risks. The solution continuously analyzes time and attendance data, identifies unusual working-hour patterns, generates actionable alerts, and supports proactive responses aligned with labor regulations and organizational policies. The presentation will cover the engineering considerations behind implementing an AI-driven monitoring solution, including data analysis approaches, anomaly detection strategies, alert design, system integration, and maintaining transparency through human oversight. It will also highlight lessons learned from introducing automation into an enterprise compliance workflow, including challenges, design decisions, and operational improvements. Attendees will gain practical insights into how machine learning can be applied to build scalable risk detection systems that improve governance, reduce manual effort, and strengthen operational processes. This session demonstrates how automation and intelligent monitoring can help teams create more reliable, secure, and efficient enterprise workflows.
