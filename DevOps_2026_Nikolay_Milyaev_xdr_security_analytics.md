# Defender XDR in the Real World: From Alert Fatigue to Measurable Risk Reduction

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Nikolay Milyaev](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Nikolay_Milyaev) (Microsoft Senior Consultant @ Microsoft). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Nikolay_Milyaev_xdr_security_analytics

## Abstract

Real-world Defender XDR playbook that turns alert storms into actionable incidents, applies safe automations to triage and contain, and cuts MTTR. Clear guidance on tuning built-ins vs custom detections and a simple metric pack to prove risk reduction.
