    Abstract

    Platform teams frequently measure availability, latency and infrastructure utilization while overlooking whether developers can successfully use the platform. A platform can meet every operational SLO and still impose days of waiting, repeated support requests and unnecessary cognitive load. This presentation introduces journey-based platform SLOs that measure the complete path from a developer’s request to a usable, policy-compliant capability. Examples include time to first environment, self-service completion rate, failed-workflow recovery time, support dependency, rework rate and time to operational readiness. Using a multi-cloud tenant-provisioning journey as a practical example, the session shows how platform teams can combine technical telemetry with developer-experience outcomes. Attendees will leave with a framework for selecting platform indicators that demonstrate whether a golden path is genuinely faster, safer and easier—not merely available.
