A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2024](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2024) by [Victor Onyenagubom](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Victor_Onyenagubom) (Lecturer in Cybersecurity @ Teesside University). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2024_Victor_Onyenagubom_security_soft_skills

## Abstract

Unlock the true potential of DevSecOps by mastering the power of soft skills! In ‘People-Powered Security,’ discover how empathy, communication, and collaboration are the unsung heroes of a security-driven culture. Learn why the human element is your greatest asset in building resilient, agile teams.
