Open the future of industrial resilience! Join me at the Chaos Engineering event for a mind-bending journey into shaping tomorrow with cutting-edge Industrial Equipment Modeling. From oil & gas processes to remote control systems, we'll explore advancements, challenges, and the persistent impacts.
