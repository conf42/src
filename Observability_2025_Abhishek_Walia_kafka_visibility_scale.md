# Observability-First Kafka: Engineering Visibility at Scale

A talk at [Conf42 Observability 2025](https://www.conf42.com/obs2025) by [Abhishek Walia](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Abhishek_Walia) (Staff Customer Success Technical Architect @ Confluent). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Abhishek_Walia_kafka_visibility_scale

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_EeN2_CWzio

## Abstract

Kafka doesn’t fail cleanly: it stalls, lags, and misfires beneath the surface. This talk cuts through the noise to show what signals actually matter, how to catch issues early, and how to make Kafka observable without drowning in metrics or noise.
