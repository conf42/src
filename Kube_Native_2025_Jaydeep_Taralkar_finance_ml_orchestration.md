# Scaling AI-Driven Financial Services: Kubernetes Orchestration for Ethical ML at Scale

A talk at [Conf42 Kube Native 2025](https://www.conf42.com/kubenative2025) by [Jaydeep Taralkar](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Jaydeep_Taralkar) (Executive Director @ UBS). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Kube_Native_2025_Jaydeep_Taralkar_finance_ml_orchestration

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z75whvnuYJA

## Abstract

Discover how Kubernetes transforms ethical AI in finance! Learn cloud-native patterns for scaling responsible ML systems that democratize financial access while maintaining regulatory compliance. Real-world architectures for fraud detection, risk assessment & financial inclusion at global scale.
