A talk at [Conf42 Machine Learning 2025](https://www.conf42.com/ml2025) by [Mohamed Najaaf](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Mohamed_Najaaf) (Solutions Architect @ AWS). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Machine_Learning_2025_Mohamed_Najaaf_guardrails_responsible_bedrock

## Abstract

Discover how Amazon Bedrock Guardrails empower organizations to build AI responsibly! In this session, we'll explore strategies to enforce ethical AI practices, mitigate risks, and ensure compliance—without compromising innovation. Join me to learn how to keep AI safe, fair, and reliable!
