# AI Systems Are Distributed Systems Now

A talk at [Conf42 Observability 2026](https://www.conf42.com/obs2026) by [Princewill Kalio](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Princewill_Kalio) (Senior Software Engineer @ Dockie). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2026_Princewill_Kalio_llms_rag_distributedsystems

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrKiXKhgf6c

## Abstract

Modern AI applications are evolving beyond prompt engineering. Today’s LLM systems are distributed architectures composed of retrieval pipelines, vector databases, tool calls, background workers, and evaluation services. Yet most teams still debug them like simple scripts. This talk explores how to apply distributed systems principles to AI applications. We will examine how tracing, telemetry, and evaluation signals can make LLM pipelines observable and debuggable. By instrumenting prompts, retrieval steps, tool calls, and model outputs as structured traces, teams can finally understand where failures occur. We will also discuss the growing role of automated evaluation pipelines in production AI systems. These systems continuously measure hallucinations, groundedness, and regression in model behavior, providing the reliability layer that modern AI infrastructure needs. Attendees will leave with a clear architecture for building observable AI systems, practical instrumentation strategies, and a deeper understanding of why the future of AI engineering looks much more like distributed systems than prompt engineering.
