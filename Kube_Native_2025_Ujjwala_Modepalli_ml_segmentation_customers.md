# Container-Native ML: Scaling Predictive Customer Segmentation on Kubernetes

A talk at [Conf42 Kube Native 2025](https://www.conf42.com/kubenative2025) by [Ujjwala Modepalli](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Ujjwala_Modepalli) (Senior Credit Risk Specialist @ Wells Fargo). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Kube_Native_2025_Ujjwala_Modepalli_ml_segmentation_customers

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykY4S6PjbHw

## Abstract

Transform your ML analytics with Kubernetes! Learn to deploy scalable, containerized customer segmentation pipelines that automatically scale, reduce costs, and handle enterprise workloads. Real-world microservices patterns for production ML systems.
