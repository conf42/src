# Enhancing IoT Cybersecurity: Leveraging Network Segmentation and Zero Trust Architecture for Robust Threat Protection

A talk at [Conf42 Internet of Things (IoT) 2024](https://www.conf42.com/iot2024) by [Amod Darshane](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Amod_Darshane) (Senior Consulting Engineer @ CDW). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Internet_of_Things_IoT_2024_Amod_Darshane_cybersecurity_network

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LARlizhpKyI

## Abstract

Discover how to fortify IoT networks with cutting-edge cybersecurity! This talk unveils the power of Network Segmentation and Zero Trust Architecture, showing how they mitigate threats, boost security by 70%, and future-proof your IoT ecosystem.
