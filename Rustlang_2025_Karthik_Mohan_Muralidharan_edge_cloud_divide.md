## Abstract

Transform enterprise IoT with Rust-powered edge computing! Learn patterns that cut response times from hours to milliseconds, reduce cloud costs 60%, and achieve 99.97% uptime. Real code, real results from 10K+ production devices. Edge+Cloud orchestration that actually works!
