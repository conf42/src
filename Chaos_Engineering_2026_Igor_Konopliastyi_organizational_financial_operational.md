# Organizational Chaos Engineering: Stress-Testing Financial & Operational Systems in Tech Companies

A talk at [Conf42 Chaos Engineering 2026](https://www.conf42.com/chaos2026) by [Igor Konopliastyi](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Igor_Konopliastyi) (Chief Operating Officer @ SPhotonix). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Chaos_Engineering_2026_Igor_Konopliastyi_organizational_financial_operational

## Abstract

Chaos engineering teaches us to intentionally inject failure into distributed systems to uncover hidden weaknesses before they cause catastrophic outages. But why do we limit this discipline to servers and microservices when tech businesses frequently fail due to operational, financial, and unit-economic breakdowns?   I apply the principles of chaos engineering and resilience testing to tech business operations and capital management. Drawing from real-world experience scaling deeptech and hardware-software enterprises, this talk demonstrates how to stress-test your business model, identify single points of failure in unit economics, and build an operationally resilient organization capable of surviving market shocks and sudden cash burn spikes. The structure of the talk: - How to apply SRE and chaos principles to financial modeling, unit economics, and operational workflows.   - Identifying "single points of failure" in team capacity, project budgeting, and plan-vs-actual execution.   - Frameworks for managing burn rate during fundraising rounds and avoiding hidden systemic risks.   - Establishing clear operational metrics that tell you when to pivot, restructure, or safely terminate unprofitable initiatives.
