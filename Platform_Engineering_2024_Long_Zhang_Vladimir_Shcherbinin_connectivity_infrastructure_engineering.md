# Beyond Connectivity: Full Life-Cycle Infrastructure Orchestration and Cost Engineering

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/platform2024) by [Long Zhang](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Long_Zhang) (Senior SRE @ Electrolux) and [Vladimir Shcherbinin](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Vladimir_Shcherbinin) (Senior SRE @ Electrolux). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2024_Long_Zhang_Vladimir_Shcherbinin_connectivity_infrastructure_engineering

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X-wMySMmHug

## Abstract

Join us to learn about our exciting shift from black-box infrastructure to self-managed empowerment for developers. Our IDP streamlines decentralization with Infrastructure as Code and a user-friendly UI, ensuring secure, scalable support for over 10 million connected appliances worldwide.
