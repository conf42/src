A talk at [Conf42 Internet of Things (IoT) 2024](https://www.conf42.com/iot2024) by [Daria Novosad](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Daria_Novosad) (Senior Product Manager @ Tele2). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Internet_of_Things_IoT_2024_Daria_Novosad_product_growth

## Abstract

Unleash IoT growth by turning insights into innovation! Learn how to use IoT analytics, gather community feedback, and validate ideas to create products users love.
