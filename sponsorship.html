How it works

From the first RSVP to years of views.

Before

  • Attendees RSVP for free on Luma
  • They join our GDPR-compliant community list: 9.100+ contacts
  • High quality leads: email, full name, job title, company, phone number & country
  • Up to 500 new contacts with every event

Kick-off day

  • Starts at 17:00 UTC with a 30-minute video call for attendees and speakers
  • All talks are pre-recorded and released at once, on YouTube and conf42.com
  • The audience talks to speakers, partners and sponsors on Discord

After

  • Every talk gets its own page, with the transcript and slides
  • Speakers get ready-made promo cards to share their talk
  • We keep promoting sessions to generate views over time
