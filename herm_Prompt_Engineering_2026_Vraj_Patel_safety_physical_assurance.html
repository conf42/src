    Abstract

    Physical AI systems in autonomous vehicles, robotics, and other safety-critical domains continue to adapt after deployment, placing pressure on traditional engineering workflows. MBSE provides a strong foundation for requirements, architecture, verification, and traceability, but recent AI-assisted tooling has focused mainly on model generation, requirement checks, and use-case discovery rather than continuous safety assurance. This session introduces a prompt engineering approach for Agentic Systems Engineering, where bounded, human-supervised AI agents support compliance monitoring, requirements traceability, hazard coverage assessment, operational design domain validation, and safety-case integrity. The MBSE model remains the single source of truth, while carefully designed prompts, structured outputs, validation rules, and escalation paths guide agent behavior. Attendees will learn how to design prompt templates for evidence retrieval, requirement-to-hazard mapping, compliance checks, and exception reporting across ISO 26262, ISO 21448, UL 4600, and ASPICE. The talk also covers practical risks, including hallucinated evidence, incomplete context, prompt drift, and over-automation, along with controls that preserve human accountability. The result is a practical architecture for using prompts and bounded agents to strengthen continuous engineering assurance for next-generation Physical AI platforms.
