    Abstract

    Modern systems generate enormous amounts of data, but collecting logs and creating dashboards alone does not guarantee visibility into system behavior. As applications become more distributed and cloud-native, teams need better ways to understand failures, performance bottlenecks, and user impact. In this session, I’ll explore how observability can be designed into modern architectures rather than added as an afterthought. We’ll discuss the role of logs, metrics, traces, and telemetry in improving operational insight and system reliability. I’ll also cover practical lessons around monitoring distributed systems, reducing troubleshooting time, and building feedback mechanisms that help teams detect and resolve issues faster. Attendees will gain practical guidance on implementing observability principles and understanding how visibility across systems can improve reliability, debugging, and operational confidence. Whether you are beginning your observability journey or improving an existing environment, this session offers practical takeaways that can be applied immediately.
