A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2025) by [Raghunadha Dornala](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Raghunadha_Dornala) (Sr Cloud Architect at @ Miracle Software Systems, Inc.). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2025_Raghunadha_Dornala_banking_load_cloud

## Abstract

Discover how an Ensemble Cloud Load-Balancing Algorithm transforms digital banking by cutting response times, boosting throughput, and ensuring secure, scalable transactions. Learn how predictive analytics and adaptive strategies keep cloud systems resilient under real-world pressure.
