# The CISO is Dead, Now What ?

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2024](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2024) by [Ilan Finkelstein](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Ilan_Finkelstein) (Principal Security Architect @ Owndata) and [Pieter VanIperen](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Pieter_VanIperen) (CISO @ Own Company). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2024_Ilan_Finkelstein_Pieter_VanIperen_ciso_dead_what

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h-XbEtPWnfE

## Abstract

Being prepared for the unexpected is the responsibility of every security and operations team, yet it is seldom practiced or simulated in a world of competing priorities. The goals are simple: how do we restore operational functionality? Getting there can be complex. How can we create simulations?
