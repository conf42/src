A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/devops2025) by [Caio Medeiros Pinto](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Caio_Medeiros_Pinto) (DevOps Human & Evangelist @ Citi). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2025_Caio_Medeiros_Pinto_environment_products_innovation

## Abstract

At your organization, how easy is for any product team ask for a new environment or either scale an existing one? It should be as fast as possible to not disturb fast flow to production.
