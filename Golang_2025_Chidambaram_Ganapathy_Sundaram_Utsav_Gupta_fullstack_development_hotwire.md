# Turbocharged Golang: Revolutionizing Full Stack Development with Hotwire

A talk at [Conf42 Golang 2025](https://www.conf42.com/golang2025) by [Chidambaram Ganapathy Sundaram](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Chidambaram_Ganapathy_Sundaram) (Senior Technologist( @ Infosys) and [Utsav Gupta](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Utsav_Gupta) (Senior Technologist @ Infosys). ## Abstract

Turbocharge your full-stack journey with Golang + Hotwire. Build lightweight, super-responsive apps by leveraging Golang’s raw power and Hotwire’s dynamic UI updates—without the burden of bulky frameworks. Enjoy a smoother coding experience that refreshes your passion for innovative development.
