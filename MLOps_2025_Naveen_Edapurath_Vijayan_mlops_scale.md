# MLOps at Scale

A talk at [Conf42 MLOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/mlops2025) by [Naveen Edapurath Vijayan](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Naveen_Edapurath_Vijayan) (Head of Engineering, AWS Central Economics @ AWS). Page: https://www.conf42.com/MLOps_2025_Naveen_Edapurath_Vijayan_mlops_scale

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xo8usJEjcIQ

## Abstract

As ML models power mission-critical applications, scalable MLOps is key to reliability and efficiency. This talk explores automating ML pipelines, CI/CD for ML, model monitoring, and governance. Learn best practices to streamline deployment and ensure responsible AI at an enterprise scale.
