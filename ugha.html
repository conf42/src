Conf42 Feature Flags 2026 is a free online conference for engineers and practitioners. All talks premiere on YouTube on the event day and stay available on demand, so you can watch them at your own pace. Here is who is presenting and what the talks are about.

Companies that presented:

Adobe, Allstate Silutions, Amazon, Bank of America, Capital One, Codeflow Capital, Confluent, Harness, Independent, Stirling, Microsoft, PayPal, SoftNet Technologies, United Therapeutics, WALMART

Making Cloud-Native Migrations Gradual, Measurable, and Reversible

A migration needs room to change course. When an entire system switches over in one release, teams have little opportunity to validate assumptions before users depend on the new architecture.
