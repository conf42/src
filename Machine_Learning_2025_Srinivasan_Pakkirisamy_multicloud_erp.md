# Mastering Multi-Cloud ERP Integration: A Framework for Data Normalization and Synchronization

A talk at [Conf42 Machine Learning 2025](https://www.conf42.com/ml2025) by [Srinivasan Pakkirisamy](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Srinivasan_Pakkirisamy) (Director @ SPL Consulting). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Machine_Learning_2025_Srinivasan_Pakkirisamy_multicloud_erp

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2xG8HzLQURA

## Abstract

As enterprises increasingly move toward multi-cloud ERP strategies, the traditional single-vendor model is being replaced by an ecosystem of specialized platforms—including Oracle Cloud ERP, Workday, Salesforce, and Blue Yonder. While this shift brings improved functionality at the departmental level, it introduces considerable complexity in data integration, consistency, and governance.
This session examines the core challenges of integrating disparate cloud-based ERP systems. Among the most significant hurdles is data normalization, where varying schema designs and inconsistent master data definitions lead to operational inefficiencies. For example, financial data must often be transformed using dozens of unique business rules, while HR platforms like Workday manage deeply relational employee records with hundreds of attributes.
Synchronization across these platforms presents additional complications. Timing discrepancies—ranging from seconds to minutes depending on transaction type—can cause data mismatches and business logic errors. Bidirectional integrations frequently lead to record conflicts, especially in high-velocity environments.
To address these challenges, I will present a structured integration framework focused on schema mapping, semantic reconciliation, synchronization conflict resolution, and data security enforcement. Enterprises adopting this approach report fewer data consistency issues than those relying on point-to-point integrations. We’ll also cover phased implementation strategies that deliver early business value and reduce risk compared to large-scale monolithic rollouts.
This session is designed for enterprise architects, integration engineers, and IT leaders looking to enable seamless operations while preserving the unique strengths of each platform in their cloud ERP stack.
