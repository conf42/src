# STEM in the Garden: How to Monitor Plants with IoT Sensors and Grafana Cloud

A talk at [Conf42 Internet of Things (IoT) 2025](https://www.conf42.com/iot2025) by [Marie Cruz](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Marie_Cruz) (Senior Developer Advocate @ Grafana Labs). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Internet_of_Things_IoT_2025_Marie_Cruz_sensors_gardening_analytics

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M5BerP-gpws

## Abstract

Are you a parent who wants to do a fun project with your kids? Are you trying to keep your plants alive? Or are you considering using an IoT device to monitor your plant's soil moisture level?

If any of the following resonates, this talk is for you!
