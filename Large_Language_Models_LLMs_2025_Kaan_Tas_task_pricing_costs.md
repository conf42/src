# AI-Driven Task Pricing: Reducing Costs in Micromobility Operations

A talk at [Conf42 Large Language Models (LLMs) 2025](https://www.conf42.com/llms2025) by [Kaan Tas](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Kaan_Tas) (General Manager @ Lime). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Large_Language_Models_LLMs_2025_Kaan_Tas_task_pricing_costs

## Abstract

Learn how AI-driven task pricing models can optimize costs and boost efficiency. Using a real-world example from Lime, discover how AI reduced warehouse task costs by over 20% through dynamic, real-time adjustments based on task density and resource demands.
