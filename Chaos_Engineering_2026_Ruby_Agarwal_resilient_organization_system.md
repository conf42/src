# Your System Is Resilient. Is Your Organization?

A talk at [Conf42 Chaos Engineering 2026](https://www.conf42.com/chaos2026) by [Ruby Agarwal](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Ruby_Agarwal) (VP of Engineering @ Avaya). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Chaos_Engineering_2026_Ruby_Agarwal_resilient_organization_system

## Abstract

The most dangerous failures are often the ones that have not fully failed yet.  Latency rises. Retries multiply. Capacity starts to tighten. Nothing is fully down, and the signals are still ambiguous.  Now the harder question appears: who decides that the system is heading in the wrong direction—and who has the authority to act before failure becomes obvious?  This session explores resilience at that boundary between technical degradation and organizational response. We’ll look at how teams recognize early failure signals, make decisions with incomplete information, and avoid the hesitation that turns a recoverable condition into a major incident.  Chaos engineering is designed to expose assumptions before production does.  One of the most important assumptions to test is whether the organization can act while there is still time.
