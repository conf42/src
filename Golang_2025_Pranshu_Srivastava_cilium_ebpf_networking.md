# The Power of Cilium For eBPF

A talk at [Conf42 Golang 2025](https://www.conf42.com/golang2025) by [Pranshu Srivastava](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Pranshu_Srivastava) (Software Engineer @ Cloudera). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Golang_2025_Pranshu_Srivastava_cilium_ebpf_networking

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kLR6hdQqmPg

## Abstract

This talk is for people who are getting started with eBPF and they don’t want to use Rust for their user space code . I will go upon my personal experience of using Cilium, why exactly did I choose it, what challenges I faced, and what mistakes people should not make.
