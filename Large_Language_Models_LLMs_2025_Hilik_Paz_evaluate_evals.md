# How to evaluate your Evals?

A talk at [Conf42 Large Language Models (LLMs) 2025](https://www.conf42.com/llms2025) by [Hilik Paz](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Hilik_Paz) (Co-founder & CEO @ Arato.ai). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Large_Language_Models_LLMs_2025_Hilik_Paz_evaluate_evals

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=osQLWrcE1Kw

## Abstract

In this session you'll learn what makes a good eval, how to define it, what are the best practices for building a test dataset, and most importantly how to make sure you can get all the way to production.
