# Andela: Bringing the future of work to life

A talk at [Conf42 Golang 2022](https://www.conf42.com/golang2022) by [Agnes Muthoni](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Agnes_Muthoni) (Director of Talent Partnerships @ Andela). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Golang_2022_Agnes_Muthoni_future_of_work_andela

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GK91Q5vLHjc

## Abstract

For technologists, establishing a career path can be a difficult process. Gaining experience and developing skills, while searching for your dream role, can take time and effort - and that's where Andela comes in! As the world's most trusted talent marketplace, Andela connects brilliance with opportunity, matching technologists across the globe to roles at exciting and innovative organizations. Agnes Muthoni, Director of Talent Partnerships at Andela, explains how you can grow your career with Andela. She will explore and discuss:

* What Andela does for technologists
* How Andela is reshaping the future of work
* Why Andela is the best place for all technologists to start their new career journey

Unlock your potential with Andela - the future of meaningful, sustainable work.

## Key takeaways

- Andela has matched thousands of technologists across the globe to their next career adventure. Now the future of work is yours to create. Anytime, anywhere. The world is at your fingertips.

- Andela is a global talent marketplace that helps companies build remote engineering teams faster. We help companies like GitHub, Goldman Sachs and Cloudfair connect with vetted remote engineers across different markets. Andela's vision of the future of work from when we were founded is now a more obvious reality.

- Andela's focus is centered on helping clients find experienced software engineers. With 175,000 technologists in our community and a successful placement rate of 96%. Remote first is changing the way we work and opening up opportunities for developers in emerging markets to enter global talent marketplace.
