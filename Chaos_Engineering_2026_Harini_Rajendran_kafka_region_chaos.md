# When a Region Fails: Chaos Engineering for Multi-Region Kafka

A talk at [Conf42 Chaos Engineering 2026](https://www.conf42.com/chaos2026) by [Harini Rajendran](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Harini_Rajendran) (Tech Lead @ Citadel). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Chaos_Engineering_2026_Harini_Rajendran_kafka_region_chaos

## Abstract

Designing a distributed system to survive failures is one thing. Proving that it can continue operating when an entire region becomes unavailable is another.  This talk explores the architecture and failure-testing strategies behind highly available, multi-region Apache Kafka deployments supporting mission-critical workloads. We'll examine how broker placement, replication factors, in-sync replica requirements, and KRaft controller quorums influence availability and durability during regional outages.  Using concrete architectural examples, we'll walk through failure scenarios including regional isolation, broker loss, controller failures, and network degradation. We'll also explore how to validate recovery behavior, maintain write availability without compromising durability, and identify failure modes that traditional monitoring may miss.  Attendees will leave with practical techniques for designing resilience experiments, evaluating failure-domain independence, and building confidence in distributed messaging systems before real incidents occur.  Key takeaways: - How to design multi-region Kafka replication and controller quorums for regional fault tolerance. - How to conduct controlled failure experiments involving brokers, network partitions, and regional outages. - How Kafka's ISR, acknowledgments, and quorum configurations influence durability and availability. - How to validate recovery, not merely detect failures. - Practical lessons for turning architectural resilience assumptions into testable guarantees.
