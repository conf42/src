# Making Cloud-Native Migrations Gradual, Measurable, and Reversible

A talk at [Conf42 Feature Flags 2026](https://www.conf42.com/featureflags2026) by [Ryan Vila](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Ryan_Vila) (Americas Advisory Director @ Harness). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Feature_Flags_2026_Ryan_Vila_feature_flags_migration

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q1nDdka4AiU

## Abstract

A migration needs room to change course. When an entire system switches over in one release, teams have little opportunity to validate assumptions before users depend on the new architecture. Feature flags create that room by separating deployment from release and allowing traffic to move gradually between existing and replacement systems.

This session examines how feature flags and experimentation support safer cloud-native migrations. We’ll cover how to introduce new services alongside legacy components, control their exposure, and use production evidence to decide when to expand a rollout, pause it, or roll it back.

We’ll look at measuring performance, cost, and reliability throughout the transition, so a successful deployment becomes the start of validation. Production testing will include operational guardrails, governance, and automated rollback, giving teams a controlled way to learn under live conditions.

Whether you’re moving workloads to the cloud, replacing APIs, or breaking a monolith into microservices, you’ll leave with a practical approach to incremental modernization: validate each change, retire legacy components as replacements prove themselves, and keep delivery moving while limiting disruption.
