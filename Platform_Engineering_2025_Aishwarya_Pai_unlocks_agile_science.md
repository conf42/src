A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/platform2025) by [Aishwarya Pai](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Aishwarya_Pai) (Senior Consultant @ Deloitte). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2025_Aishwarya_Pai_unlocks_agile_science

## Abstract

58% of ML platforms fail to deliver value. Discover how platform engineering unlocks 68% faster model delivery and 37% higher AI success rates through cloud-native architecture and automated pipelines.
