A talk at [Conf42 Prompt Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/prompt2025) by [Shashank Shivam](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Shashank_Shivam) (Senior Software Engineer @ Oracle). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Prompt_Engineering_2025_Shashank_Shivam_models_psychology_behavior

## Abstract

Unlock the hidden psychology behind AI responses! In this mind-bending talk, discover how prompts shape model behavior like human thought using framing, priming, and reasoning tricks. Learn to influence LLMs not just with words, but with psychological precision.
