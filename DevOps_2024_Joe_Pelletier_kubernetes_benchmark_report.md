## Abstract

The 2024 Kubernetes Benchmark Report is out! Join us as we review the results of over 150,000 scanned workloads to learn what's working and what needs to be improved. Join to see how you compare and get advice on what you need to do, and what you don't need to do.
Key Takeaways:
Benchmark yourself against others, see what you are doing well and what you need to improve, and get past issues that may be limiting your ability to gain the full value of Kubernetes.
