    Abstract

    Many organisations start their CDC journey with just a few pipelines. Then more teams adopt it, new use cases emerge, and the platform team ends up spending its time reviewing configurations, deploying connectors, troubleshooting issues, and handling tickets. The problem is no longer CDC itself, but how it can scale. Debezium Platform addresses this challenge by providing an abstraction around sources, destinations, transformations, and pipelines. Platform teams can offer a self-service experience that allows data engineers to create and manage pipelines, while Debezium Platform takes care of deployment, operations, monitoring, and observability. In this talk, we’ll explore how Debezium Platform helps standardise CDC adoption across teams and provides a consistent way to deploy, operate, and observe pipelines at scale. In the live demo, we’ll see how data from a single database can be consumed by three teams with different needs. We’ll create and deploy pipelines for each use case and see how the platform manages their lifecycle, health, and observability from a single place.
