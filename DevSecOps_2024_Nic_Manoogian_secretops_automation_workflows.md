# The Power of SecretOps: Automating Secrets Workflows

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2024](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2024) by [Nic Manoogian](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Nic_Manoogian) (Head of Engineering @ Doppler). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2024_Nic_Manoogian_secretops_automation_workflows

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rWoNKcoEQdo

## Abstract

Modern trends in app development focus on code and compute, often overlooking the importance of secrets and configuration management. Let’s explore “SecretOps,” a layered framework for automating secrets workflows.
