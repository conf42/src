# Securing the AI Perimeter: Building Zero-Trust Integration Patterns for AI-Driven Pricing in SAP

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2026) by [Sirisha Ayyagari](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sirisha_Ayyagari) (Software Developer @ Software Developer). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2026_Sirisha_Ayyagari_zerotrust_sap_pricing

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K2-KFfvy9cY

## Abstract

Enterprises racing to embed AI into core financial systems face a hard constraint: innovation cannot come at the cost of system integrity, auditability, or financial control. Pricing engines sit inside the Order-to-Cash pipeline, and any external AI integration must be secured, governed, and clean-core compliant from day one — not retrofitted after a breach or an audit failure.  This session presents a production-tested architecture for securely integrating an external AI pricing engine with enterprise SAP systems, built around a hard security boundary: SAP retains sole authority over transactional consistency and financial validation, while the AI engine is confined to a stateless recommendation role with no write access to core financial objects. Integration is implemented through HTTP-based APIs and SAP BAdI extension points, with zero modification to standard SAP objects  preserving upgrade safety, auditability, and a minimal attack surface by design.  We walk through the DevSecOps practices that made this deployment safe to ship: securing and rate-limiting the AI-to-SAP API boundary, validating and sanitizing every model output before it touches a financial transaction, enforcing least-privilege access between the AI layer and SAP, and building in circuit breakers and rollback paths so a misbehaving model can never directly alter pricing, discounting, or revenue data. We also cover the governance layer: logging and traceability for every AI-influenced pricing decision, to satisfy internal audit and regulatory review.  Attendees will leave with a concrete blueprint for threat-modeling AI integrations into core ERP systems, practical ABAP-side guardrail patterns, and a replicable security architecture for teams under pressure to adopt AI without compromising the financial controls their business depends on.
