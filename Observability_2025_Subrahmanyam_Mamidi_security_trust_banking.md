# Observability in the Cloud: Enhancing Security and Trust in Modern Banking

A talk at [Conf42 Observability 2025](https://www.conf42.com/obs2025) by [Subrahmanyam Mamidi](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Subrahmanyam_Mamidi). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Subrahmanyam_Mamidi_security_trust_banking

## Abstract

Unlock the power of observability in cloud banking! Learn how real-time insights boost security, ensure compliance, and build trust in financial systems. Discover how to detect threats early and respond faster—because in banking, visibility isn't optional, it's essential.
