# Boosting System Resilience with Exponential Backoff in Distributed Computing

A talk at [Conf42 Rustlang 2024](https://www.conf42.com/rustlang2024) by [Suraj Modi](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Suraj_Modi) (Software Engineer @ Uber). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Rustlang_2024_Suraj_Modi_system_resilience_backoff_computing

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=koRM1AqjZkU

## Abstract

Boost your distributed systems' resilience with exponential backoff! Learn how this dynamic strategy reduces network congestion by 40%, improves response times by 30%, and decreases downtime by 25%. Discover its impact on cloud platforms, microservices, and databases.
