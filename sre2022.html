Conf42 Site Reliability Engineering 2022 is a free online conference for engineers and practitioners. All talks premiere on YouTube on the event day and stay available on demand, so you can watch them at your own pace. Here is who is presenting and what the talks are about.

Companies that presented:

3Cloud, Anova, API7.ai, Applitools, Autify, AWS, Blameless, Cortex, Cyren, Dynatrace, FireHydrant, Formidable, Google, Gremlin, incident.io, last9.io, LinkedIn, Logz.io, Mulesoft, NGINX, O(1) Labs, Site24x7, Zoho, Slim.ai, SPS Commerce, Styra, T-Systems International, Tailscale, Teleport, VictoriaMetrics, Weave, Wix, Xellentro
