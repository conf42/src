Thursday, September 26

Editions 2022 2023 2024 2025

Conf42 Kube Native 2024 is a free online conference for engineers and practitioners. All talks premiere on YouTube on the event day and stay available on demand, so you can watch them at your own pace. Here is who is presenting and what the talks are about.

Companies that presented:

Amadeus, Amazon, Ariga, AWS, CircleCI, Flox, Fresenius Medical Care, GlassFlow, Google, HYR Global Source, Kasten by Veeam, Kubermatic, Lowes, MetalBear, Microsoft, Netflix, Planview, Reailize, a B-Yond company, Rialtic, Senser, Signet Jewelers, Sysdig, The Trevor Project, TrueFullstaq, Wix, WSO2, Zilliz
