# From Data to Decisions: Bridging AI and IoT for Smarter System

A talk at [Conf42 Internet of Things (IoT) 2024](https://www.conf42.com/iot2024) by [Natalia Drazhnik](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Natalia_Drazhnik) (Product owner @ YCH Blue Digital Limited). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Internet_of_Things_IoT_2024_Natalia_Drazhnik_sata_decisions_smarter_system

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vADfUR2B2Bg

## Abstract

AI is reshaping brand reputation management by turning social media data into actionable insights. My talk explores leveraging AI for sentiment analysis and trend detection to enhance customer engagement and build a reputation-focused, customer-first strategy.
