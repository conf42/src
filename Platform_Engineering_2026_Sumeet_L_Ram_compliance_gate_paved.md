# The Paved Road Had a Compliance Gate

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2026](https://www.conf42.com/platform2026) by [Sumeet L. Ram](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sumeet_L_Ram) (Senior Technical Product Manager @ PayPal). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2026_Sumeet_L_Ram_compliance_gate_paved

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SlNLDiAkxyI

## Abstract

Platform teams measure success in adoption. Mine is used by more than 150 product teams across 200 markets. Adoption was the easy part. The hard design problem was deciding what the platform would refuse to do. I own a Model Context Protocol server at PayPal that exposes three tools in front of the internationalization layer. Product teams use it to get correct address formats, name order, official government identifiers and ISO 20022 message structure for a market without first becoming experts in that market. An integration that used to take a two-week sprint now takes three hours. This talk covers the part of platform engineering that does not fit the golden-path story: what happens when your consumers are AI agents instead of engineers, and the paved road has to carry a compliance gate. Permission scoping per market. The failure modes we hit when a tool call was confidently correct about the wrong market. And how you show an auditor what a self-service platform did two years ago.
