A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2023](https://www.conf42.com/devops2023) by [Pawel Piwosz](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Pawel_Piwosz) (Lead Systems Engineer @ EPAM Systems). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2023_Pawel_Piwosz_cicd_enterprise_level

## Abstract

CI/CD seems to be simple. But let's take a step back, and look at it from a helicopter view.
Let's think about the design CI/CD processes for the project, team, even the whole company. Let's go through "architecture of CI/CD". What areas should we cover? How to talk with Stakeholders about CI/CD when we design the backbone of DevOps driven Organization?
