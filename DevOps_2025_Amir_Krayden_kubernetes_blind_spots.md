# Kubernetes Minus the Blind Spots

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/devops2025) by [Amir Krayden](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Amir_Krayden) (Co-Founder & CPO @ Senser). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2025_Amir_Krayden_kubernetes_blind_spots

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ibS4mKCrYCk

## Abstract

In this session, we'll share practical strategies for combining topology insights with existing observability tools to pinpoint issues faster and resolve them with confidence.
