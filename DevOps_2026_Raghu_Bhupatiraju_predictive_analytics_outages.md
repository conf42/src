# Predictive Observability: Prevent Supply Chain Outages Before They Break Your Production

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Raghu Bhupatiraju](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Raghu_Bhupatiraju) (Senior Engineer @ Expeditors, USA). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Raghu_Bhupatiraju_predictive_analytics_outages

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fFrllgJv7Kk

## Abstract

Stop waking up at 3 AM to production fires you could've prevented. Learn how predictive analytics + real-time observability detects supply chain disruptions hours before they crash your systems. Real architectures, real results, practical DevOps patterns you can implement Monday.
