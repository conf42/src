# From Chaos to Clarity: Simplifying Cloud Management with a Personal Touch

A talk at [Conf42 Cloud Native 2025](https://www.conf42.com/cloud2025) by [Denish Tomar](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Denish_Tomar) (Developer Relations Engineer @ Qumulus). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Cloud_Native_2025_Denish_Tomar_management_simplicity_clarity

## Abstract

Tired of juggling complex cloud setups? Join me as I share my personal journey to simplify cloud management. Learn how I turned chaos into clarity, discovering a flexible, predictable, and scalable solution that allowed me to focus on building, not managing. Ready for stress-free cloud scaling?
