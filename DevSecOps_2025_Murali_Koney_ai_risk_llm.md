# Securing Enterprise AI: Governance, Risk, and Economics of LLM Adoption

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2025) by [Murali Koney](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Murali_Koney) (Technical Team Lead @ Cox Automotive). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2025_Murali_Koney_ai_risk_llm

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z2BM2KeumGo

## Abstract

Most enterprise AI projects fail before production. This talk reveals how DevSecOps leaders can secure, govern, and scale LLM adoption with a proven framework balancing cost, compliance, and performance for sustainable enterprise AI success.
