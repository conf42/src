# DevOps, 12-Factor, and Platform Engineering

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/platform2024) by [Justin Reock](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Justin_Reock) (Head of DevRel @ Cortex). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2024_Justin_Reock_devops_12factor_engineering

## Abstract

Learn about the business wisdom of the 70s and 80s and how it laid the foundation for modern, cloud-native systems incorporating DevOps practices. Get a glimpse into what's next by exploring internal developer portals.
