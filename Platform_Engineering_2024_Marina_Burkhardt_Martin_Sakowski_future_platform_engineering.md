# The Future of Platform Engineering

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/platform2024) by [Marina Burkhardt](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Marina_Burkhardt) (Global Solutions Architect @ AWS) and [Martin Sakowski](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Martin_Sakowski) (Senior Solutions Architect @ AWS). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2024_Marina_Burkhardt_Martin_Sakowski_future_platform_engineering

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CJYDT6acbPM

## Abstract

This talk explores the future of platform engineering, focusing on abstractions, standards, characteristics, and mechanisms. It discusses a decentralized platform approach and various platform levels.
