# Navigating Security in the Subscription Economy: Strategies, Metrics, and Compliance for Sustainable Growth

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2024](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2024) by [Kiran Nagubandi](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Kiran_Nagubandi) (Solutions Architect @ SPL Consulting). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2024_Kiran_Nagubandi_subscription_economy_security

## Abstract

Discover how to thrive in the booming subscription economy while staying secure and compliant! Learn key strategies, models, and metrics, and explore DevSecOps practices to protect customer data and drive growth. Gain actionable insights on secure billing.
