# Algorithmic Transparency in Action: Strengthening AI-Driven Incident Management

A talk at [Conf42 Incident Management 2025](https://www.conf42.com/im2025) by [Vasanth Mudavatu](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Vasanth_Mudavatu) (Senior Principal Software Engineer - Gen AI, ML & Application Security Products @ Dell Technologies). ## Abstract

Can you trust your AI during a breach? Learn how algorithmic transparency boosts incident resolution, reduces false positives, and streamlines forensics. Discover practical tools and frameworks that make your AI-driven response both powerful and explainable.
