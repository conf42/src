# Quantum Leap: Revolutionizing Rust Software Development with Quantum Computing

A talk at [Conf42 Rustlang 2024](https://www.conf42.com/rustlang2024) by [Abhinav Chunchu](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Abhinav_Chunchu) (Senior Software Engineer @ Lowes Inc). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Rustlang_2024_Abhinav_Chunchu_quantum_software_development_computing

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LClZOMWb5-E

## Abstract

Experience the quantum leap in Rust development! Discover how quantum computing's qubits, superposition, and entanglement can revolutionize software paradigms. Explore cutting-edge quantum languages, hybrid models, and groundbreaking applications in cryptography, optimization, and AI. Join us to unlock the future of technology!
