# How to Build Cloud-Native Platforms with Kubernetes

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2024](https://www.conf42.com/platform2024) by [Piotr Zaniewski](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Piotr_Zaniewski) (Head of Engineering Enablement @ Loft Labs). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2024_Piotr_Zaniewski_cloudnative_kubernetes_platforms

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HQNeAZUXzbk

## Abstract

Platform Engineering and SRE focus on empowering developers and organizations by creating and maintaining internal software products known as platforms. In this blog, we will explore what platforms are, why they are important, and uncover best practices for well-architected platforms.
