# The AI Trust Triad for DevOps: Shipping Reinforcement Learning, Federated Learning, and Explainable AI Safely in Regulated Systems

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Saideepak Kandibanda](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Saideepak_Kandibanda) (RPA Solutions and Automation Manager @ DISH NETWORK LLC). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Saideepak_Kandibanda_trust_explainability_regulation

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdSYFwGj2t4

## Abstract

AI in production is hard. Learn how DevOps teams can safely ship Reinforcement Learning, Federated Learning, and Explainable AI in regulated finance and supply chains using real-world pipelines for trust, privacy, observability, and continuous delivery.
