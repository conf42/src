# From Lift-and-Shift to Kubernetes: A Two-Phase DevOps Modernization

A talk at [Conf42 DevOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/devops2026) by [Harris Baskaran](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Harris_Baskaran) (Staff Site Reliability Engineering @ Google Inc., USA). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevOps_2026_Harris_Baskaran_kubernetes_modernization_containers

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NKsyUwfwtsI

## Abstract

A battle-tested DevOps story: move a high-volume ticketing monolith from VMware/Oracle to GCE, then rebuild cloud-native on GKE + PostgreSQL—zero downtime tactics, dependency mapping, replication, Kubernetes security policies, and multi-region DR lessons.
