# From Agents to Systems: Orchestration, Recovery, and Economics

A talk at [Conf42 MLOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/mlops2026) by [Rajesh Gupta](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Rajesh_Gupta) (Head of Agentic AI - Product @ Skan AI). Page: https://www.conf42.com/MLOps_2026_Rajesh_Gupta_agents_orchestration_economics

## Abstract

Agentic AI shows strong promise in BFSI, but production deployments surface challenges that go far beyond model performance. This talk shares real-world lessons from deploying multi-agent systems in regulated environments, focusing on three critical areas: reliable orchestration across legacy systems, recovery through verification and repair agents, and the economics of autonomous execution. It explains why separating planning, execution, authorization, and recovery is essential for compliance and resilience, and how non-linear cost behavior in agentic systems requires budget guardrails and outcome-based evaluation to deliver predictable ROI in production.
