# Observability: Seeing Through the Chaos of Modern Systems

A talk at [Conf42 Observability 2025](https://www.conf42.com/obs2025) by [Naga Murali Krishna Koneru](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Naga_Murali_Krishna_Koneru) (Technical Architect @ Hexaware Technologies). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Naga_Murali_Krishna_Koneru_chaos_modern_systems

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yFUfDdD46OY

## Abstract

Monitoring shows you're broken - observability tells you why. In distributed systems, you need traces beyond logs/metrics to debug fast. Learn how to build truly observable systems that turn outages into insights.
