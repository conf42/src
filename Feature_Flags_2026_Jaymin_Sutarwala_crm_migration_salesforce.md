# De-Risking a Legacy CRM Migration in Life Sciences: Feature-Flag-Driven Rollout Strategy for a Governed Salesforce Patient Engagement Platform

A talk at [Conf42 Feature Flags 2026](https://www.conf42.com/featureflags2026) by [Jaymin Sutarwala](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Jaymin_Sutarwala) (Associate CRM Strategy and Operations Director @ United Therapeutics Corporation). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Feature_Flags_2026_Jaymin_Sutarwala_crm_migration_salesforce

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xfA_Y5pGfM0

## Abstract

United Therapeutics needed to retire Keystone Entrada, a legacy patient CRM, and cut over patient communications to a new Salesforce-based ecosystem spanning Marketing Cloud, Data Cloud, and Health Cloud  without disrupting live patient engagement or risking PHI/PII exposure during the transition. A big-bang cutover was not an option given the compliance stakes and the volume of active patient touchpoints running through the legacy system.  This session shares how feature-flag-driven rollout strategy was used to control the blast radius of the migration: gating identity resolution and data unification behind flags as each source system was onboarded, toggling segmentation and activation logic into Marketing Cloud incrementally rather than all at once, and running the old and new HUB/copay ingestion pipelines in parallel behind flags to validate data integrity before fully retiring the legacy path.  Attendees will learn how progressive rollout patterns applied to a regulated, PHI-sensitive environment reduced the risk of duplicate records and broken personalization mid-migration, how flag-gated cutover supported governed, auditable rollback points at each stage, and how this approach let the team validate campaign performance and triggered communications on the new platform before fully decommissioning vendor dependencies. The talk translates feature-flag migration patterns more commonly seen in pure engineering contexts into a regulated, patient-data-heavy environment where governance and auditability carry as much weight as rollout speed.
