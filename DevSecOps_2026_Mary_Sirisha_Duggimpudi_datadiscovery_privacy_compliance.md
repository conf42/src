# AI-Driven Sensitive Data Discovery for Secure DevSecOps and Privacy Compliance

## Abstract

Modern DevSecOps environments rarely keep sensitive data in one place. Enterprise applications distribute regulated information across cloud services, APIs, operational databases, analytics platforms, logs, and hybrid infrastructure, making manual discovery and periodic compliance reviews difficult to sustain. This session presents a practical approach to AI-driven sensitive data discovery that integrates metadata intelligence, semantic analysis, runtime telemetry, and automated governance workflows into modern DevSecOps practices.  The presentation explores, through practical engineering scenarios, how engineering teams can design discovery pipelines that continuously identify sensitive data, classify risk, and surface compliance issues as systems change. It examines practical architecture and design choices for combining machine learning with deterministic policies, metadata signals, application context, and operational telemetry, while addressing challenges such as false positives, explainability, changing schemas, and distributed ownership. The session also covers how discovery findings can trigger automated workflows for validation, remediation, and evidence collection, connecting security and privacy controls with everyday DevSecOps processes.  Attendees will gain practical, reusable patterns for building continuous sensitive-data visibility, integrating discovery into delivery and operational workflows, and improving privacy risk detection without relying solely on manual audits. The session ultimately concludes with lessons for evolving toward intelligent, automated governance and stronger operational resilience across distributed systems.
