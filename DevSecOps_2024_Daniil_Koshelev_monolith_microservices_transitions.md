# From Monolith to Microservices: A Guide to Seamless Transitions

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2024](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2024) by [Daniil Koshelev](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Daniil_Koshelev) (Senior Software Engineer @ Tinkoff). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2024_Daniil_Koshelev_monolith_microservices_transitions

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qisIalSZQSk

## Abstract

Discover the secrets to successfully migrating from monolithic systems to microservices, ensuring security, scalability, and seamless integration.
