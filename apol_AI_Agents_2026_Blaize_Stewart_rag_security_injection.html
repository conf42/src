    Abstract

    Retrieval-augmented generation makes AI applications more useful, but it also creates a new security boundary: retrieved content becomes part of the model’s working context. In this session, we will intentionally break a simple RAG application using synthetic data and safe demos. We will look at direct prompt injection, poisoned documents, broken document-level authorization, unsafe tool use, insecure output handling, and data leakage through logging and citations. Then we will rebuild the app with practical mitigations: identity-aware retrieval, document provenance, ingestion scanning, prompt isolation, source attribution, output validation, scoped tools, human approval for sensitive actions, and automated adversarial tests. The goal is not to scare people away from RAG, but to show how to design RAG apps as real enterprise systems with trust boundaries, policy enforcement, and observable controls.
