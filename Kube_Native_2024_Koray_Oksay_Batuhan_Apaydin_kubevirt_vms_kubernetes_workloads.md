# KubeVirt Alchemy: Transforming VMs into Kubernetes Workloads

A talk at [Conf42 Kube Native 2024](https://www.conf42.com/kubenative2024) by [Koray Oksay](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Koray_Oksay) (Kubernetes Consultant @ Kubermatic) and [Batuhan Apaydin](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Batuhan_Apaydin) (Organizer KCD Turkey & Istanbul Cluster). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Kube_Native_2024_Koray_Oksay_Batuhan_Apaydin_kubevirt_vms_kubernetes_workloads

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XH96hrbCWqo

## Abstract

According to the CNCF Annual Survey 2023, only 39% said they use containers "for most or all production applications." This indicates that many organizations have only partially migrated their applications to containers or have not even started. KubeVirt is a technology that bridges this gap.
