# Faster, Cheaper, Smarter: Lessons from Building Zero-Instrumentation Observability with Fluent Bit OpenSearch & Prometheus

A talk at [Conf42 Observability 2025](https://www.conf42.com/obs2025) by [Isala Piyarisi](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Isala_Piyarisi) (Observability Engineer @ WSO2) and [Nilushan Costa](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Nilushan_Costa) (Associate Technical Lead @ WSO2). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Observability_2025_Isala_Piyarisi_Nilushan_Costa_fluent_opensearch_zero

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DswnTMsGk1E

## Abstract

We rebuilt our platform's observability using Fluent Bit, OpenSearch & Prometheus, improving user experience with <5s log latency and ~70% cost savings. This talk explores our migration, achieving zero-instrumentation telemetry via eBPF and lessons building a faster, cheaper OSS stack.
