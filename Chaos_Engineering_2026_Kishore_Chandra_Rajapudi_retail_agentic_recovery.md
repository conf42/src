# Engineering Self-Healing Retail Systems with Agentic AI and Failure Recovery

A talk at [Conf42 Chaos Engineering 2026](https://www.conf42.com/chaos2026) by [Kishore Chandra Rajapudi](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Kishore_Chandra_Rajapudi) (Senior Software Engineer @ 7-Eleven). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Chaos_Engineering_2026_Kishore_Chandra_Rajapudi_retail_agentic_recovery

## Abstract

Distributed retail platforms depend on interconnected services for ordering, payments, inventory, loyalty programs, and delivery. When failures occur, traditional monitoring often generates alerts without coordinating remediation or validating recovery. This session explores how Agentic AI can strengthen operational resilience through intelligent failure detection, policy-driven remediation, and controlled service restoration.  The session presents a proposed Autonomous Retail Operations Agent built around five stages: detecting abnormal service behavior, recommending corrective actions, executing policy-approved temporary store disablement, enabling human-initiated recovery, and validating system health before reopening.  Using a representative scenario involving sustained transaction failures, attendees will explore how event streaming, AI-assisted diagnosis, deterministic policy checks, and automated notifications can coordinate incident response. The architecture addresses challenges such as distinguishing isolated errors from persistent failures, reducing alert fatigue, preventing transactions during disruptions, and avoiding premature recovery.  The discussion also examines how health validation, role-based permissions, and auditable recovery policies support safer automation in distributed environments. Attendees will gain practical architectural insights into designing bounded AI agents for resilient retail operations, with future considerations including predictive failure detection, cross-store correlation, and digital twins.
