# From Dashboard to Defense: Automating Resilience at Large Scale

A talk at [Conf42 Platform Engineering 2025](https://www.conf42.com/platform2025) by [Sureshkumar Karuppuchamy](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sureshkumar_Karuppuchamy) (Engineering Lead @ eBay). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Platform_Engineering_2025_Sureshkumar_Karuppuchamy_defense_dashboard_large

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L5Y4Wt3yP4o

## Abstract

Go beyond dashboards: see how we engineered adaptive rate limiting, auto-rollbacks, circuit breakers, and closed-loop pipelines to build a self-healing, high-scale listing platform that keeps billions of transactions resilient — with zero manual firefighting.
