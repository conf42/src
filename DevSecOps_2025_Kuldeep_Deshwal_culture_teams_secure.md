# Sustainable Developer Productivity: Avoiding Pitfalls in DevSecOps Culture

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2025) by [Kuldeep Deshwal](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Kuldeep_Deshwal) (Director of Engineering @ Proofpoint). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2025_Kuldeep_Deshwal_culture_teams_secure

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e7rOpdfQLJM

## Abstract

Discover how to overcome the “productivity paradox” in DevSecOps. Learn the top pitfalls draining developer effectiveness and a proven framework to boost secure delivery, reduce burnout, and build sustainable, high-performing engineering cultures.
