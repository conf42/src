# Engineering Resilient Data Pipelines for Commissions and Trade Intelligence

A talk at [Conf42 Chaos Engineering 2026](https://www.conf42.com/chaos2026) by [Sridhar V Potunuru](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Sridhar_V_Potunuru) (Principal Integration Engineer @ Bright Horizons). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Chaos_Engineering_2026_Sridhar_V_Potunuru_pipelines_commissions_data

## Abstract

In financial data systems, pipeline failures, data inconsistencies, and processing delays can directly affect commission payouts, trade reporting, and regulatory compliance. Building resilient data architectures is essential to maintaining accuracy, reliability, and operational continuity.  This session explores practical approaches to engineering fault-tolerant data pipelines for complex commissions and trade environments. Attendees will learn how modern ELT architectures using platforms like Snowflake organize raw, refined, and curated data layers to support reliable processing, reconciliation, and near real-time reporting.  The discussion examines common engineering challenges, including high-volume trade processing, complex commission hierarchies, missing or inconsistent records, and failures in data transformation workflows. It highlights validation frameworks, exception handling, reconciliation processes, and data lineage tracking as essential techniques for detecting issues, limiting their impact, and maintaining data integrity.  The session also covers modernizing legacy ETL systems such as DataStage while preserving critical financial business logic. Emphasis is placed on observability, scalable architecture, governance, and collaboration across engineering, finance, and compliance teams.  Attendees will gain practical insights into designing reliable, auditable, and maintainable data pipelines that reduce operational risks and support accurate financial reporting.
