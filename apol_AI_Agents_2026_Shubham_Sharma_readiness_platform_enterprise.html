    Abstract

    Enterprise AI agent platforms increasingly integrate foundation models, retrieval systems, orchestration frameworks, cloud infrastructure, security services, enterprise applications, and human-in-the-loop workflows, requiring coordinated readiness across multiple technical and operational domains. However, organizations frequently evaluate these capabilities independently, resulting in fragmented governance, inconsistent deployment decisions, and increased operational risk. This paper presents the Mission-Critical Readiness Framework (MCRF) as a unified governance model for AI agent ecosystems, integrating technical, operational, infrastructure, security, organizational, and AI-specific readiness into a comprehensive lifecycle framework. MCRF establishes standardized readiness principles, measurable governance mechanisms, and structured assessment processes that evaluate agent reliability, model governance, tool connectivity, workflow resilience, observability, compliance, and operational preparedness before production deployment. By providing a common readiness model across engineering, AI, platform, security, and operations teams, the framework improves collaboration, strengthens governance, and enables evidence-based decisions throughout the AI agent lifecycle. As organizations deploy increasingly autonomous and interconnected intelligent systems, MCRF offers a practical, scalable, and repeatable methodology for achieving predictable, measurable, and trustworthy readiness across mission-critical AI agent platforms while improving delivery confidence, operational resilience, and long-term system reliability.
