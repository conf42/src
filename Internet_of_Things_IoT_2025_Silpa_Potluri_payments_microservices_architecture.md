# IoT Payment Systems: Microservices Architecture for Connected Devices

A talk at [Conf42 Internet of Things (IoT) 2025](https://www.conf42.com/iot2025) by [Silpa Potluri](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Silpa_Potluri) (Software Developer @ GoodLeap). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Internet_of_Things_IoT_2025_Silpa_Potluri_payments_microservices_architecture

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3-cU8kmiNaU

## Abstract

Learn how payment gateways evolved from monoliths to microservices to handle billions of IoT device transactions, enabling real-time processing with 99.99% uptime and 40% cost reduction through scalable, cloud-native architectures.
