# Enhancing Healthcare Chatbot Transparency with Explainable AI (XAI): Addressing Security and Ethical Challenges

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2024](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2024) by [Abhishek Vajpayee](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Abhishek_Vajpayee) (Senior Analytics Engineer/Product Owner @ Metropolis Technologies) and [Rathish Mohan](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Rathish_Mohan) (Senior Applied ML Scientist @ Lore | Contagious Health). ## Abstract

Discover how Explainable AI (XAI) is transforming healthcare chatbots by making them transparent, secure, and ethically sound. Learn how to tackle data privacy and security challenges in DevSecOps while delivering context-aware, empathetic care.
