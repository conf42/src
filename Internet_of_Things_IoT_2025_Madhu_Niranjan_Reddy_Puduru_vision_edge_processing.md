# Edge AI for Smart Camera IoT Devices: Real-Time Image Processing Without Cloud Dependencies

A talk at [Conf42 Internet of Things (IoT) 2025](https://www.conf42.com/iot2025) by [Madhu Niranjan Reddy Puduru](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Madhu_Niranjan_Reddy_Puduru) (Software Engineering Manager @ Synchronoss Technologies). Page: https://www.conf42.com/Internet_of_Things_IoT_2025_Madhu_Niranjan_Reddy_Puduru_vision_edge_processing

## Abstract

Transform dumb IoT cameras into intelligent edge devices! Learn how to deploy neural networks on $10 hardware, process images in real-time without cloud, save bandwidth, extend battery life, and protect privacy. From smart doorbells to industrial vision - make your IoT cameras think locally!
