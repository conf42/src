# Building Unbreakable Systems: Risk-Driven DevSecOps in Action

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2025) by [Nishanth Sirikonda](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Nishanth_Sirikonda) (Workday Solutions Architect @ First Day Foundation). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2025_Nishanth_Sirikonda_risk_secure_system

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nvq6a3Vnygw

## Abstract

Systems fail when risk is overlooked. This talk shows how risk-driven DevSecOps — powered by automation, monitoring, and compliance-as-code — helps enterprises build resilient, “unbreakable” systems that adapt under pressure without slowing innovation.
