# AI for Inclusive Education: Secure and Ethical Personalization of Learning Pathways

A talk at [Conf42 DevSecOps 2025](https://www.conf42.com/devsecops2025) by [Raghav Sai Cheedalla](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Raghav_Sai_Cheedalla) (Senior Software Engineer @ Meta). Page: https://www.conf42.com/DevSecOps_2025_Raghav_Sai_Cheedalla_ai_ethics_edu

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5lmBGWbej3o

## Abstract

AI is reshaping education for 7.5M+ U.S. students with special needs. This session shows how DevSecOps safeguards data, ensures equity, and powers adaptive learning systems that improve outcomes while keeping trust and compliance at the core.
