# Shipping Agentic AI to Production: What a Regulated Enterprise Learned the Hard Way

A talk at [Conf42 MLOps 2026](https://www.conf42.com/mlops2026) by [Shanker Valipireddy](https://www.conf42.com/speaker_Shanker_Valipireddy) (Principal Architect / AI Enablement Lead @ Aflac). Page: https://www.conf42.com/MLOps_2026_Shanker_Valipireddy_agentic_regulated_enterprise

## Abstract

A practitioner talk on moving agentic AI from demos to production in a regulated enterprise: per-agent identity and least-privilege access, risk-adaptive policy gates that auto-approve low-risk actions and escalate high-risk ones, immutable audit, and evaluation as a platform-enforced MLOps gate — grounded in public agent benchmarks and production failure patterns, plus 20 years of platform work including a 20,000-pipeline migration. Takeaway: a reference pattern — identity, scoped grant, policy gate, audit — and a checklist for operating agents in production.
