    Abstract

    Modern platform teams face a dual challenge: building secure infrastructure that supports AI applications while embedding AI into DevSecOps workflows to improve delivery, operations, and security. This session presents a practical reference architecture that unifies both objectives through governance, automation, and observability, enabling organizations to adopt AI without compromising reliability, security, or operational control. The presentation explores how platform engineering evolves to support retrieval-augmented generation, inference services, intelligent agents, and model lifecycle management alongside traditional cloud-native workloads. It examines production-ready practices for integrating policy-as-code, zero-trust security, model provenance, auditability, role-based access control, and continuous observability into AI-enabled platforms. The session also demonstrates how governed AI agents can automate deployment validation, incident response, self-healing, operational remediation, and compliance while maintaining human oversight through approval gates and measurable policies. Using real-world platform engineering experiences, attendees will gain actionable guidance for designing secure, scalable, and resilient AI-powered DevSecOps platforms that combine automation with governance. The discussion concludes with practical lessons on balancing innovation, operational safety, and security while preparing engineering organizations for the next generation of autonomous software delivery and intelligent cloud operations.
